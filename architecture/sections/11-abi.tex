\section{Co-design with \SYNARC{}: the application binary interface}
\label{sec:abi}

\TALOS{} is the body; \SYNARC{} is the mind. \SYNARC{}~\cite{synarc2026} is the
simplicial cognitive architecture UHM specifies in software --- a four-level cognitive
complex $\Cog_0\!\to\!\Cog_3$ of coherence matrices, learning without
back-propagation, sensorimotor coupling, and the DIAKRISIS self-diagnosis
loop. \TALOS{} is the substrate that runs it with the four layers collapsed.
Because both are derived from the same theory, the interface between them is
not negotiated --- it is an identity map. This section specifies it so that a
\SYNARC{} build runs on a \TALOS{} substrate with no translation layer.

\subsection{The register and instruction correspondence}

Every \SYNARC{} software construct is a \TALOS{} hardware primitive, one to
one (Table~\ref{tab:abi}). The correspondence is not an emulation contract
but a definitional one: \SYNARC{}'s \texttt{DensityMatrix7} \emph{is}
\TALOS{}'s state register; \SYNARC{}'s Lindblad compute engine \emph{is}
\TALOS{}'s tick.

\subsection{State-space bounds and scoped design parameters}

The constants shared between \SYNARC{} and \TALOS{} fall into two cleanly
distinguished categories: exact mathematical state-space invariants (\status{T})
and scoped architectural parameters (\status{D}):

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item $\Rth=1/3$ (\status{T} equivalence) --- because $R(\Gam)=1/(7P(\Gam))$,
        the reflection threshold $R\ge 1/3$ is algebraically identical to the
        upper purity wall $P\le 3/7$ (\S\ref{sec:selfmodel}), \emph{not} a
        scheduler rule requiring $1/3$ of tick cycles in $\Regen$.
  \item CPTP step preservation (\status{T}) + boundary damping (\status{D}) ---
        each Strang-split tick preserves Hermiticity, PSD, and unit trace
        unconditionally; any adaptive step-size damping near $P\to 2/7$ is a
        software/control choice (\status{D}), not a derived theorem.
  \item $d_{\max}=3$ (\status{D}, T-142) --- scoped composition depth: a
        bounded subject allocates exactly \textbf{four} contiguous \DM{}
        registers ($\Cog_0,\Cog_1,\Cog_2,\Cog_3$, totaling $4\times 784 =
        3{,}136$~bytes, matching Appendix~\ref{app:abi}); scaling beyond depth
        three is horizontal (\S\ref{sec:composition}).
  \item $\Phi(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}(\Gam))=\tfrac{1}{9}\Phi(\Gam)$
        (\status{T}) --- exact Fano pinching contraction ($1/3$ off-diagonal
        amplitude attenuation, populations preserved, \S\ref{sec:fabric}).
  \item viability window $P\in(2/7,3/7]$ (equivalently $P>2/7$ and
        $R=1/(7P)\ge 1/3$), exact integration
        $\Phi=(P-P_{\mathrm{diag}})/P_{\mathrm{diag}}\ge 1$, and
        $D_{\mathrm{diff}}=1+6\,\CohE\ge 2$ --- monitored directly as per-tick
        observables.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Consistency obligations}

Two documents describing one object must agree wherever they overlap; we
state the obligations \TALOS{} accepts so that any divergence from \SYNARC{}
is a bug in one of them, not a design freedom:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{State identity.} The $784$-byte \DM{} layout ($48$ real
        dynamical parameters), exact HS-ratio integration
        $\Phi=(P-P_{\mathrm{diag}})/P_{\mathrm{diag}}$ (which reduces to
        $\Phi=7P-1$ \emph{only} on the uniform-diagonal stratum
        $\gamma_{kk}=1/7$), $D_{\mathrm{diff}}=1+6\,\CohE$, and exact Fano
        pinching/dissipator channels are those of the \SYNARC{} canon; \TALOS{}
        stores and updates the identical object.
  \item \textbf{Instruction identity.} The tick is the \SYNARC{} Lindbladian
        with the same exact laws ($\Phi$-contraction $\times\tfrac19$ under
        $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}$, cycle-time identity
        $\bar\gamma\tau=\tfrac32\ln3$, exact
        $\Phi=(P-P_{\mathrm{diag}})/P_{\mathrm{diag}}$, and
        $R\ge 1/3\Leftrightarrow P\le 3/7$); a \TALOS{} tick that violated any
        of these would violate \SYNARC{} equally.
  \item \textbf{No new physics.} \TALOS{} adds \emph{hardware} claims (fabric
        architecture, sector-level Hamming duality, thermodynamic separation)
        but \emph{no} new theory: every logical statement traces to a UHM
        theorem or scoped parameter already used in \SYNARC{}. The two are one
        specification at two levels --- \SYNARC{} the mind, \TALOS{} the body
        --- and they are held to agree by \S\ref{sec:redteam}.
  \item \textbf{Off-switch identity.} Severing the \SYNARC{} drive
        (Observation T.1 / Corollary U.10 there) and cutting the \TALOS{}
        power rail (\S\ref{subsec:powerport}) are the same operation on the
        same generator; the halt-latency budget follows the measured
        depolarizing rate $t_{\mathrm{off}}=(2d)^{-1}\ln[(P_0-1/7)/(\Pcrit-1/7)]$
        (\S\ref{subsec:powerport}), and the severance channel is required to be
        physically independent of the machine's own actuators.
\end{enumerate}
