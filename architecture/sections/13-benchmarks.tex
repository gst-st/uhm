\section{Falsifiable engineering claims}
\label{sec:benchmarks}

An architecture earns attention only with measurements that could go against
it. We reduce \TALOS{} to four benchmarks, each against a von~Neumann
baseline running the \emph{same} adaptive workload, each with an explicit
pass/fail. Failure of any is a failure of the corresponding claim at its
stated scope.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering\small
\caption{The four falsifiable claims. Each is measured against a von~Neumann
baseline executing the identical adaptive task; ``rung'' names the lowest
realisability rung (\S\ref{sec:realizability}) at which the claim is expected
to hold.}
\label{tab:benchmarks}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.5cm}p{3.5cm}p{3.9cm}p{3.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{\#} & \textbf{Claim} & \textbf{Pass} & \textbf{Fail / rung} \\
\midrule
B1 & Energy per adaptive step tracks \emph{adaptation rate}, not reversible work &
    measured energy/step falls as the holon converges toward $\rhostar$ at fixed unitary work &
    energy flat or rising with unitary work / in-memory or analogue rung \\
B2 & Sector-level fault localisation via dual parity matrix $H$ &
    all $7$ single-sector faults uniquely decoded by $s=He_j^T\pmod 2$ ($3$ dual checks, no routing table; distinct from physical bit-ECC) &
    ambiguous syndrome or routing table required / any rung \\
B3 & Exact Fano pinching contraction $\Phi\to\Phi/9$ and $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Fano}}=\tfrac23\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{atom}}$ &
    $\Phi(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}(\Gam))/\Phi(\Gam)=1/9$ across general (non-uniform diagonal) states &
    ratio $\ne 1/9$ or populations altered / any rung (arithmetic) \\
B4 & Bounded-tower horizontal scaling ($d_{\max}=3$, T-142 \status{D}) &
    per-agent state stays fixed at $3{,}136$~B ($4$ registers) while ecology throughput scales linearly in $N$ &
    verification footprint or failure domain grows super-linearly with $N$ / any rung \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Three of the four are already discharged at the V0 emulation rung. \textbf{B2}
is the exact Hamming $[7,4,3]$ / simplex $[7,3,4]$ duality ($HM^T\equiv 0\pmod 2$,
verified across all $7$ single-sector faults in
\texttt{architecture/talos\_v0\_emulator.py} and Appendix~\ref{app:fano}).
\textbf{B3} is exact Fano pinching arithmetic
($\Phi(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}(\Gam))=\Phi(\Gam)/9$,
$\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Fano}}=\tfrac23\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{atom}}$, verified
across arbitrary non-uniform diagonal states in
\texttt{architecture/talos\_v0\_emulator.py} and
\texttt{website/scripts/check\_core\_numbers.py}). \textbf{B4} is demonstrated
by the vectorized SIMT ecology batch ($N$ independent $784$-B holons scaling
linearly in memory and throughput while each tower stays bounded at $3{,}136$~B).
\textbf{B1} --- the physical energy-scaling claim --- requires a substrate above
the digital GPU rung to test, because digital CMOS switching and data movement
mask the thermodynamic floor; it remains the primary experimental target and the
honest open item.
