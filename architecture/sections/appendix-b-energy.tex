\section{Thermodynamic entropy accounting, derived}
\label{app:energy}

We derive Eq.~\eqref{eq:energy-law} and clarify its exact scope. Landauer's
principle~\cite{landauer1961} states that reducing a system's entropy by $1$~bit
($\ln 2$~nats) while exporting it to a thermal bath at temperature $T$ requires
at least $k_B T\ln 2$ of free energy. The logical thermodynamic budget of a
tick is therefore bounded below by $k_B T\ln 2$ times the bits of von~Neumann
entropy exported.

\paragraph{The three terms.} Decompose the tick (Eq.~\ref{eq:lomega}), measuring
von~Neumann entropy in bits, $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gam)=-\Tr(\Gam\log_2\Gam)$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Unitary} $-i[H_{\mathrm{eff}},\Gam]$: unitary conjugation
        $U\Gam U^\dagger$ preserves all eigenvalues identically, so
        $\dot S_{\mathrm{unitary}}=0$ exactly. It exports zero logical entropy,
        the same bound that applies to any logically reversible
        computation~\cite{bennett1973}.
  \item \textbf{Dissipator} $\Diss$: the Fano Lindblad dissipator
        $\gamma(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}(\Gam)-\Gam)$ (and optional
        depolarization) \emph{raises} $S_{\mathrm{vN}}$ at rate
        $\dot S_{\Diss}>0$ bits/time. Holding a coherent state against this leak
        requires re-exporting $\Delta H_{\Diss}^{(+)}=\dot S_{\Diss}\Delta\tau$
        bits per tick --- the maintenance term.
  \item \textbf{Regeneration} $\Regen$: driving $\Gam$ toward the anchored
        self-model $\varphi_J(\Gam)$ \emph{lowers} $S_{\mathrm{vN}}$ by
        $\Delta H_{\Regen}$ bits per tick (creating order), requiring at least
        $k_B T\ln 2\cdot\Delta H_{\Regen}$ of free energy.
\end{itemize}
Summing gives $E_{\mathrm{tick}}\ge k_B T\ln 2\,(\Delta H_{\Regen}+\Delta H_{\Diss}^{(+)})$,
Eq.~\eqref{eq:energy-law}.

\paragraph{Exact numbers ($T=300$~K) and honest scope.}
At $T=300$~K, $k_B T\ln 2 = 1.380649\!\times\!10^{-23}\cdot 300\cdot\ln 2
= 2.87\times 10^{-21}$~J ($0.018$~eV per bit). For $\Gam\in\Dens(\mathbb{C}^7)$,
the exact maximum von~Neumann entropy is attained at $\Gam=\mathbb{1}/7$:
\[
S_{\max} \;=\; \log_2 7 \;\approx\; 2.807355\;\text{bits}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
E_{\max,\text{state}} \;=\; k_B T\ln 7 \;=\; (\log_2 7)\,k_B T\ln 2
\;\approx\; 8.060\times 10^{-21}\;\text{J}.
\]
Note carefully that $S_{\max}=\log_2 7\approx 2.807$~bits is the exact
von~Neumann entropy ceiling of a $7$-dimensional density matrix --- \emph{not}
$48$~bits (which is the real manifold dimension of $\Dens(\mathbb{C}^7)$) and
\emph{not} $6{,}272$~bits (the IEEE-754 digital storage representation of
$49$ \texttt{complex128} entries). All bit-entropy calculations and
step-frequency-independent dissipation rates are verified directly in
\texttt{architecture/talos\_v0\_emulator.py}. We do \emph{not} divide physical
CMOS DRAM joules by this theoretical Landauer floor: Landauer bounds apply
equally to any logically reversible machine, whereas CMOS power is dominated by
switching capacitance and leakage. On digital hardware, \TALOS{}'s immediate
energy benefit is keeping the $784$-byte state resident in L1/registers to
avoid off-chip DRAM fetches.
