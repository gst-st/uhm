\section{Energy: the machine pays for learning, not for computing}
\label{sec:energy}

\subsection{Where a von Neumann machine spends}

After Dennard scaling ended, the energy of a computation is dominated by
\emph{moving} its operands, not by the arithmetic: a floating-point operation
costs $\sim\!1$~pJ, an on-chip word $\sim\!1$~pJ, and an off-chip DRAM word
$\sim\!20$~pJ --- one to two orders more, and rising with distance. A
learning machine compounds this: training shuttles activations and gradients
between compute and memory on every step. The von~Neumann energy floor is a
\emph{data-movement} floor, and it sits some seven to eight orders of
magnitude above the thermodynamic (Landauer) limit~\cite{landauer1961,bennett1973} of the same computation.

\subsection{Where \TALOS{} spends: a new accounting}

The one instruction of \TALOS{} splits cleanly into a reversible part and two
entropy-exporting parts (Table~\ref{tab:three-terms}), and this split induces
an energy law with no counterpart in a layered machine.

\begin{claimbox}[Logical entropy--learning law \status{T} for the unitary core, \status{C} for the physical coefficient]
The unitary term $-i[H_{\mathrm{eff}},\Gam]$ evolves $\Gam$ by unitary
conjugation $U\Gam U^\dagger$: it preserves the spectrum and the von~Neumann
entropy $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gam)=-\Tr(\Gam\log_2\Gam)$ identically, so its
logical Landauer entropy export is \emph{zero}. All logical entropy export is
charged to the two non-unitary terms:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:energy-law}
E_{\mathrm{tick}} \;\geq\; k_B T\ln 2 \,\cdot\, \Delta H_{\mathrm{exported}},
\qquad
\Delta H_{\mathrm{exported}} \;=\; \underbrace{\Delta H_{\Regen}}_{\text{learning (bits)}}
\;+\; \underbrace{\Delta H_{\Diss}^{(+)}}_{\text{maintenance (bits)}},
\end{equation}
where $\Delta H_{\Regen}$ (in bits) is the von~Neumann entropy reduction
created by regeneration and $\Delta H_{\Diss}^{(+)}$ (in bits) is the entropy
that must be re-exported to hold the state against the dissipator's leak.
\emph{At the logical thermodynamic floor, reversible unitary work exports zero
entropy; only maintenance and learning require entropy export.}
\end{claimbox}

Two limiting cases make the accounting concrete.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{A holon at its fixed point} ($\Gam=\rhostar$, converged, not
        learning) has $\Delta H_{\Regen}=0$; its only logical thermodynamic
        cost is the maintenance term needed to hold $\rhostar$ against
        dissipation. If the dissipator is switched off, pure unitary evolution
        exports zero entropy.
  \item \textbf{A holon that is learning} exports logical entropy in proportion
        to the order it creates per tick toward its self-model, tracking
        adaptation rather than reversible unitary work.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The numbers and honest thermodynamic scope}

At $T=300$~K, the Landauer cost per irreversibly erased or exported bit is
$k_B T\ln 2 = 2.87\times10^{-21}$~J ($0.018$~eV). For a seven-mode density
matrix $\Gam\in\Dens(\mathbb{C}^7)$, the exact maximum von~Neumann entropy is
\begin{equation*}
S_{\max} \;=\; \log_2 7 \;\approx\; 2.807\;\text{bits}
\qquad (\ln 7 \approx 1.946\;\text{nats}),
\end{equation*}
attained at $\Gam=\mathbb{1}/7$. Consequently, the worst-case single-state
thermodynamic floor --- creating a pure state from $\mathbb{1}/7$ in one step
--- is
\[
k_B T\ln 7 \;=\; (\log_2 7)\,k_B T\ln 2 \;\approx\; 8.06\times10^{-21}\;\text{J}
\;\approx\; 0.050\;\text{eV}.
\]
It is essential not to conflate three distinct numbers: (i) the von~Neumann
state entropy $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gam)\le\log_2 7\approx 2.807$~bits, (ii) the
real manifold dimension ($48$ continuous parameters), and (iii) the digital
emulation storage size ($49\times 128 = 6{,}272$ IEEE-754 bits $= 784$~bytes).

\noindent\textbf{No apples-to-oranges CMOS comparison.} We explicitly do
\emph{not} divide physical off-chip CMOS DRAM energy ($\sim\!2\times10^{-11}$~J/word)
by the theoretical Landauer bound ($\sim\!10^{-20}$~J) to claim a ``nine-order''
silicon energy advantage. Reversible thermodynamic bounds apply equally to
\emph{any} logically reversible machine (including Bennett-reversible
von~Neumann circuits~\cite{bennett1973}), whereas the actual power of any CMOS
realisation is governed by physical transistor switching energy, capacitance,
clocking, and leakage. \TALOS{} offers two separate, cleanly scoped engineering
facts: (1) on digital substrates, keeping the entire $784$-byte state resident
in local registers/L1 removes off-chip DRAM fetches per tick (a memory-hierarchy
locality gain, not a Landauer gain); and (2) on future physically reversible or
driven-dissipative substrates (\S\ref{sec:realizability}), separating the
entropy-preserving unitary step from dissipation and regeneration isolates the
irreversible thermodynamic cost to adaptation and maintenance.

\begin{mythbox}[The myth, continued --- ichor]
The giant's walking cost him nothing --- bronze does not tire. What the ichor
paid for was subtler: staying \emph{bronze} --- bright, whole, un-corroded
against the salt wind --- and getting \emph{better} at the patrol. That is
the energy law in one image: computation is the free walking; maintenance and
learning are the only two things the vein must pay for
(Eq.~\ref{eq:energy-law}).
\end{mythbox}

\begin{zerobox}[From zero --- why the toy needs feeding]
A wind-up toy runs down: not because moving is expensive, but because springs
leak. This machine's ``spring'' leaks too (the forgetting term), so it must
be fed --- that is the port. The surprise is what \emph{doesn't} cost: the
thinking itself. Feed it, and the thinking rides along free; starve it, and no
cleverness inside can keep it wound (\S\ref{subsec:powerport}). Its
food-bill tracks two things only: how much order it is \emph{holding}, and
how fast it is \emph{learning}.
\end{zerobox}

\subsection{The metabolic floor and the operating-point axis}
\label{subsec:metabolic}

The law above accounts for \emph{computation} (reversible, Landauer-free) and
\emph{learning} (the decaying $\Regen$ term). One term remains: the cost of
merely \emph{staying alive}. The dissipator $\Diss$ continually pushes $\Gam$
toward the maximally mixed heat-death state $I/7$; holding the state against
this leak is not free.

\begin{claimbox}[The metabolic floor \status{T}$+$\status{C} (T-273)]
At steady state a viable coherent holon ($P>2/7$, $\Phi\ge 1$) exports
von~Neumann entropy at the dissipator's strictly-positive rate
$\dot S_{\Diss}>0$ (measured in bits per unit time; T-271). By Landauer, the
minimum thermodynamic \emph{maintenance} power is
\[
  P_{\mathrm{meta}} \;\geq\; k_B T\ln 2\cdot \dot S_{\Diss} \;>\; 0
  \qquad (\dot S_{\Diss}\text{ in bits/time}),
\]
the \emph{active, irreversible} counterpart of the reversible unitary core. A
viable dissipative holon \textbf{cannot run for free}. Because $\dot S_{\Diss}$
is a \emph{physical} entropy-production rate (per unit continuous time $\tau$,
not per numerical step $\Delta\tau$), this floor is
\textbf{step-frequency-independent} (T-276): refining $\Delta\tau$ improves
integration accuracy without changing the physical entropy-production rate. It
is directly measurable as a live telemetry observable (\S\ref{sec:platform}).
\end{claimbox}

The free energy that pays this floor has exactly one entry point: the
\textbf{power port} of \S\ref{subsec:powerport}. The drive
$\Lind_{\mathrm{feed}}$ imports structured negentropy from the environment;
regeneration \emph{directs} it into maintained coherence but cannot create it
from a unital closed map (T-288) --- so an unplugged machine cannot pay
$P_{\mathrm{meta}}$ and halts, and a plugged one pays it out of the negentropy
flux $r$ delivers. Maintenance power, the ignition threshold $(r/\bar\gamma)_c$,
and the halt latency after severance are three faces of one budget line.

The floor scales with the \textbf{order maintained} (the negentropy
$\log_2 7 - S_{\mathrm{vN}}$ in bits): a more coherent state has stronger
dissipator entropy production and costs more to hold --- \emph{the price of
complexity}. This turns the viability window into an explicit \textbf{design
axis} rather than a single set-point.

\begin{claimbox}[The operating-point axis \status{C} (T-274)]
The viable window $(2/7,3/7]$ is a capability--efficiency axis, not one point:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Lean edge $P\to 2/7$} --- efficiency-optimal: minimal maintenance
        power, minimal margin. The ``survival'' mode --- highest
        capability-per-watt (for $C=\Phi\times R$), least robust.
  \item \textbf{Rich edge $P\to 3/7$} (where $R=1/(7P)=1/3$; on the
        uniform-diagonal stratum $\Phi=7P-1=2$ and $C=\Phi R=2/3$) ---
        capability-optimal at higher maintenance power. The ``thriving'' mode.
\end{itemize}
An engineer can tune the operating point across $(2/7,3/7]$ to trade margin and
coherence against maintenance export. The optimal set-point is \status{C} (it
depends on the capability measure chosen); what is \emph{robust} is the
monotone thermodynamic cost of maintaining higher coherence. Numerically
verified in \texttt{architecture/talos\_v0\_emulator.py}.
\end{claimbox}

\begin{claimbox}[The efficiency law \status{T}$+$\status{C} (T-276)]
Two refinements complete the thermodynamic budget. \textbf{(i)~Step-frequency
independence \status{T}.} The metabolic floor is
$P_{\mathrm{meta}} \ge k_B T\ln 2\cdot\dot S_{\Diss}$, set by the
\emph{physical} entropy-production rate in bits/time: per-tick entropy
production scales linearly with $\Delta\tau$, so the rate is independent of
tick discretization frequency.
\textbf{(ii)~Order-per-joule \status{T}.} Creating order (reducing
$S_{\mathrm{vN}}$ by $1$~bit) costs $\geq k_B T\ln 2$, so thermodynamic
regeneration efficiency
$\eta = (k_B T\ln 2\cdot\text{bits ordered})/(\text{free energy spent}) \leq 1$
is Landauer-bounded. \textbf{(iii)~Speed--efficiency tradeoff \status{C}.}
$\eta\to 1$ only in the \emph{quasi-static} limit and falls under fast
finite-time driving (irreversible dissipation). The engineering rule follows:
\textbf{run regeneration as slowly as the dissipator leak permits} for maximal
order-per-joule --- the coherent-machine analogue of a slow, near-reversible
heat engine. Verified numerically in \texttt{architecture/talos\_v0\_emulator.py}.
\end{claimbox}
