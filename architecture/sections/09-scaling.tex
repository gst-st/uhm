\section{Expressiveness and scaling: bounded towers and horizontal ecologies}
\label{sec:scaling}

Section~\ref{sec:composition} introduced a bounded vertical discipline: each
unified cognitive tower is capped by the scoped design parameter $d_{\max}=3$
(four registers $\Cog_0$--$\Cog_3$, $3{,}136$~bytes; T-142 \status{D}). That
design cap raises the immediate engineering question: \emph{when per-agent
register width and nesting depth are fixed, how does system capacity scale?}
At the single-register level, enlarging the normed division algebra past the
octonions is algebraically obstructed: the Cayley--Dickson tower
$\mathbb{R}\!\to\!\mathbb{C}\!\to\!\mathbb{H}\!\to\!\Oct$ ends at $\Oct$,
because the next step (the sedenions) has zero divisors
($(\mathrm{e}_3+\mathrm{e}_{10})(\mathrm{e}_6-\mathrm{e}_{15})=0$ with
nonzero factors), breaking unique invertibility (Theorem~\ref{thm:unique},
\cite{baez2002}).

Accordingly, \TALOS{} separates two distinct statements: (i)~the proven
algebraic bounds of octonionic Jordan and projective geometry (\status{T}),
which serve only as a structural analogy (\status{I}) for bounded single-subject
depth rather than a dynamical symmetry of coupled registers, and (ii)~the real
engineering scaling axis, which is \textbf{unbounded horizontal scaling across
peer holon ecologies}.

\subsection{The octonionic Jordan reference ceiling (structural analogy)}
\label{subsec:jordan}

Self-adjoint observables of a quantum-like system form a \emph{Jordan algebra}
under the symmetric product $a\circ b=\tfrac12(ab+ba)$. For a single holon's
seven modes, the underlying octonionic structure constants $f_{ijk}$ on
$\mathrm{Im}(\Oct)$ carry the continuous automorphism group
$G_2=\mathrm{Aut}(\Oct)$ ($\dim 14$), while the fixed-basis Fano fabric and
dissipator on $\Dens(\mathbb{C}^7)$ are covariant only under the discrete
collineation group $\mathrm{PSL}(2,7)$ (order~$168$) and diagonal phase
rephasing $U(1)^7$ (\S\ref{sec:collapse}; all $48$ real parameters of $\Gam$
are dynamically active).

\begin{theorem}[Jordan--von~Neumann--Wigner ceiling \status{T}]
\label{thm:jordan-ceiling}
The $n\times n$ octonionic Hermitian matrices $\mathcal{H}_n(\Oct)$ form a
formally real Jordan algebra \textbf{if and only if $n\leq 3$}
(Jordan--von~Neumann--Wigner~\cite{jordan1934}): for $n\geq 4$ octonion
non-associativity breaks the Jordan identity. At $n=3$ this yields the
exceptional Albert algebra $\Alb$ ($\dim 27$) with automorphism group
$F_4=\mathrm{Aut}(\Alb)$ ($\dim 52$) and reduced structure group $E_6$
($\dim 78$).
\end{theorem}

\noindent\textbf{Honest separation of status.} Theorem~\ref{thm:jordan-ceiling}
itself is established mathematics (\status{T}, \cite{jordan1934,baez2002}).
By contrast, pairing the Jordan-rank limit $n\le 3$ with the scoped
architectural nesting cap $d_{\max}=3$ (T-142 \status{D}, \S\ref{sec:composition})
is strictly a \textbf{structural analogy} (\status{I}), \emph{not} a proven
dynamical $F_4$ or $E_6$ symmetry of coupled \DM{} registers. In actual
multi-holon registers (such as tensor products $(\mathbb{C}^7)^{\otimes k}$ or
the four-register tower $\Cog_0$--$\Cog_3$), state dimension grows by direct
register composition without inducing an exceptional $F_4$ or unital spin-factor
action (verified numerically in \texttt{website/scripts/check\_core\_numbers.py},
\texttt{no\_unital\_spin\_factor\_on\_any\_holon\_register}), and UHM's own
no-reduction theorem T-220 blocks reducing a $7$-mode base ($\mathbb{C}P^6$,
$\chi=7$) to an $F_4$ base ($\OPtwo$, $\chi=3$). Earlier universal claims tying
depth three to tricategorical coherence breakdown (T-217) or continuous
$G_2$-rigidity (T-42a) were withdrawn and are not used here.

\subsection{Terminal projective plane over division algebras}
\label{subsec:terminal}

\begin{theorem}[Terminal division-algebra projective plane \status{T}]
\label{thm:terminal}
The octonionic projective plane $\OPtwo$ (real dimension $16$, rank-one
idempotents of $\Alb$, isometry group $F_4$) is the \textbf{terminal}
projective geometry over any normed division algebra: no $\Oct P^n$ exists for
$n\geq 3$, because projective geometry of dimension $\geq 3$ satisfies
Desargues' theorem, forcing an associative coordinate ring, whereas $\Oct$ is
non-associative~\cite{baez2002}.
\end{theorem}

\noindent Geometrically, $\Fano$ is the smallest finite projective plane
(seven points, order two, directly implemented as the \TALOS{} fabric of
\S\ref{sec:fabric}--\ref{sec:ecc}), whereas $\OPtwo$ is the largest
division-algebra projective plane (\status{T} mathematics; cited here solely as
an algebraic geometry landmark, not as a claim that a multi-register hardware
tower carries an $F_4$ dynamical state manifold).

\subsection{The actual architectural scaling law}
\label{subsec:scaling-law}

System capacity scales across \emph{two} sharply separated regimes:
\textbf{(I) Per-subject tower --- bounded (\status{D}).} A single holon is one
$784$-byte \DM{} register ($48$ real dynamical parameters, discrete
$\mathrm{PSL}(2,7)\ltimes U(1)^7$ fabric covariance); a composed subject nests

in parallel), coordinated over the \textsc{Fanos} peer-to-peer fabric

\end{claimbox}

\subsection{Sensorimotor coupling across tiers}
\label{subsec:sensorimotor}

Sections~\ref{sec:fabric}--\ref{sec:selfmodel} treated the holon's internal
tick; sensorimotor coupling closes the loop without adding a separate control
layer (UHM T-100--T-104, T-159). External observations enter $\Gam$ through the
encoding map \textsc{Enc} (modulating the dissipative/input channels), while
motor commands are read out via \textsc{Dec} (driving the Hamiltonian/actuator
interface to reduce the largest resource deficit
$\max_k\sigma^{\mathrm{motor}}_k$). Across tiers this gives a clean engineering
hierarchy without invoking speculative exceptional-group biomechanics:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Single-holon node ($784$~B).} A compact seven-channel
        sensorimotor controller (e.g.\ an actuator node, drone controller, or
        instrument loop) running in-place CPTP updates with continuous
        sector-level syndrome telemetry (\S\ref{sec:ecc},~\ref{sec:selfmodel}).
  \item \textbf{Bounded multi-register body ($3{,}136$~B).} A four-register
        tower ($\Cog_0$--$\Cog_3$) coordinating multiple sensorimotor channels
        within one L1-resident state block and fixed verification budget.
  \item \textbf{Federated ecology ($N$ nodes).} Large multi-agent systems,
        distributed robotics, and \SYNARC{}-scale deployments scale horizontally
        as peer ecologies coupled over \textsc{Fanos} (K.5,~\cite{synarc2026}),
        not by inflating a single monolithic register.
\end{itemize}
