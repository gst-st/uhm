\section{Glossary and UHM cross-reference}
\label{app:glossary}

\paragraph{Terms.}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt,itemsep=1pt]
  \item[\DM{}] the state register: $\Gam\in\Dens(\mathbb{C}^7)$, $784$~bytes ($48$ real dynamical parameters).
  \item[Tick] one application of $\LindOmega$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:lomega}); the sole instruction.
  \item[Line-bus] one of the seven canonical Singer Fano lines; the interconnect unit (\S\ref{sec:fabric}).
  \item[Mediator $k^\ast(i,j)$] the unique third sector of a pair's Fano line; deterministic reroute.
  \item[Collapse] the derivation of interconnect, sector-level Hamming duality, instruction support, and Fano-pinched self-model from one incidence $\mathsf{N}$ (\S\ref{sec:collapse}).
  \item[Impedance] the emulation overhead a substrate adds to run the logical architecture (\S\ref{sec:realizability}).
  \item[Power port] the non-unital drive $\Lind_{\mathrm{feed}}=r(\rho_{\mathrm{env}}-\Gam)$; the rail importing negentropy whose severance causes predictable decay toward $\mathbb{1}/7$ (\S\ref{subsec:powerport}).
\end{description}
