\section{ABI details: layout and tick pseudocode}
\label{app:abi}

\paragraph{State layout.} A holon is a contiguous $784$-byte block ($48$ real
dynamical parameters); a bounded subject (scoped nesting depth $d_{\max}=3$,
T-142 \status{D}, \S\ref{sec:composition}, \S\ref{sec:abi}) allocates exactly
\textbf{four} contiguous \DM{} registers --- base level $\Cog_0$ plus three
nested meta-levels $\Cog_1$--$\Cog_3$:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}rll@{}}
\toprule
offset & size & field \\
\midrule
$0$    & $784$ & \texttt{gamma} : \DM{} ($49$ complex \texttt{f64}, LE) --- base level $\Cog_0$ \\
$784$  & $784$ & meta-level $\Cog_1$ \DM{} \\
$1568$ & $784$ & meta-level $\Cog_2$ \DM{} \\
$2352$ & $784$ & meta-level $\Cog_3$ \DM{} \\
\midrule
       & $3136$ & total bounded subject state ($4$ registers, fits in L1) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
This is byte-identical to the \SYNARC{} \texttt{gamma}/\texttt{rho\_ep} layout:
a \SYNARC{} checkpoint is a \TALOS{} state image and vice versa, with no
marshalling.

\paragraph{Tick (per register).}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\texttt{tick($\Gam$, $\Delta\tau$):}} \\
\quad\texttt{$\Gam \leftarrow$ U\_half($\Gam$, $\Delta\tau/2$)} & // unitary, spectrum- \& entropy-preserving \\
\quad\texttt{$s \leftarrow$ syndrome($\Gam$)} & // $3$-bit dual $H$ check (App.~\ref{app:fano}, Prin.~\ref{prin:selfdiag}) \\
\quad\texttt{$\Gam \leftarrow$ dissip\_fano($\Gam$, $\Delta\tau$)} & // $\gamma(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}(\Gam)-\Gam)$ ($+$ optional depol.) \\
\quad\texttt{$\Gam \leftarrow$ regen\_phiJ($\Gam$, $\Delta\tau$)} & // toward $\varphi_J(\Gam)=(1-R)\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}(\Gam)+R\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}$ \\
\quad\texttt{$\Gam \leftarrow$ U\_half($\Gam$, $\Delta\tau/2$)} & // Strang symmetry $+$ PSD projection \\
\quad\texttt{assert invariants($\Gam$)} & // Hermitian, $\Tr{=}1$, PSD; exact $\Phi=(P-P_{\mathrm{diag}})/P_{\mathrm{diag}}$ \\
\quad\texttt{return ($\Gam$, $s$)} & \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The returned $3$-bit syndrome $s = He_j^T\pmod 2$ feeds the DIAKRISIS check on
the \SYNARC{} side; a non-zero $s$ uniquely localises a single degraded sector
(Appendix~\ref{app:fano}). Per-tick telemetry evaluates exact
$\Phi=(P-P_{\mathrm{diag}})/P_{\mathrm{diag}}$ (which reduces to $7P-1$
\emph{only} on uniform diagonals $\gamma_{kk}=1/7$), $R=1/(7P)$ (where
$R\ge 1/3 \Leftrightarrow P\le 3/7$ is the upper purity bound of the viability
window), $\CohE=(\gamma_{EE}^2+2\sum_{j\neq E}|\gamma_{Ej}|^2)/P$,
$D_{\mathrm{diff}}=1+6\,\CohE$, and $S_{\mathrm{vN}}=-\Tr(\Gam\log_2\Gam)\le\log_2 7$~bits.
Any adaptive step-size damping near the viability boundary is a software control
choice (\status{D}) rather than a derived theorem.
