\section{The tick, cycle-accurate}
\label{app:tick}

One \TALOS{} tick advances $\Gam\leftarrow\Gam+\Delta\tau\,\LindOmega[\Gam]$
by a structure-preserving Strang split that keeps $\Gam$ a legal density
matrix (Hermitian, PSD, unit trace) to machine precision:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Unitary half-step} $e^{-i H_{\mathrm{eff}}\Delta\tau/2}$:
        logically reversible; preserves eigenvalues and von~Neumann entropy
        identically (zero logical entropy export). Cost $\sim\!700$~FLOPs for
        one \DM{} multiply; arithmetic depth $\log_2 7\approx 2.8$.
  \item \textbf{Dissipator + regeneration full step} using the Fano-sparse
        kernel: the Fano Lindblad dissipator is
        $-\tfrac{\gamma}{6}\sum_{p=1}^7[\Pi_p,[\Pi_p,\Gam]]
        = \gamma(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}(\Gam)-\Gam)
        = \tfrac{2}{3}\gamma(\operatorname{diag}(\Gam)-\Gam)$ over the seven
        rank-$3$ line projectors $\Pi_p$ (preserving populations and damping
        off-diagonals at rate $2\gamma/3$, plus optional depolarization
        $\gamma_{\mathrm{dep}}(\mathbb{1}/7-\Gam)$ when modelling full reset);
        regeneration drives $\Gam$ toward the canonical nonlinear anchored
        self-model $\varphi_J(\Gam)=(1-R(\Gam))\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}(\Gam)
        + R(\Gam)\ket{\psi}\bra{\psi}$.
  \item \textbf{Unitary half-step} again (Strang symmetry, second-order
        accurate in $\Delta\tau$), followed by positive-semidefinite
        projection.
\end{enumerate}
Per-tick cost is $\sim\!1{,}400$~FLOPs (two unitary halves) plus the sparse
Fano pinching/regeneration step. All operands are the resident $784$-byte state
($48$ real dynamical parameters); no memory beyond L1 is touched. The natural
cycle time obeys $\bar\gamma\tau=\tfrac32\ln3$ (\S\ref{sec:substrate}).

\noindent\textbf{Exact invariants checked in
\texttt{architecture/talos\_v0\_emulator.py}:} Fano pinching contraction
$\Phi(\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{Fano}}(\Gam))=\tfrac19\Phi(\Gam)$; exact
integration $\Phi=(P-P_{\mathrm{diag}})/P_{\mathrm{diag}}$ (reducing to
$\Phi=7P-1$ \emph{only} on uniform diagonals $\gamma_{kk}=1/7$);
$R=1/(7P)\ge 1/3\Leftrightarrow P\le 3/7$; Hermiticity, unit trace, and
positivity; discrete $\mathrm{PSL}(2,7)\ltimes U(1)^7$ covariance; and $3$-bit
Hamming $[7,4,3]$ single-sector syndrome decoding.
