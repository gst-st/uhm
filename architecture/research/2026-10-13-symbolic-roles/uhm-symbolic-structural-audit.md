## 1. Genuine structural bridges

1. **Trigrams and F2^3.** Once line order and yin/yang encoding are fixed, a trigram is exactly an element of F2^3. Linewise toggling is XOR. This is an isomorphism of the expressly chosen Boolean action structure; it does not establish equivalence of divinatory interpretations. Removing000 gives the7 projective points. Each pair of distinct nonzero vectors determines the line `{a,b,a+b}`. There are7lines and21pairs, each on one line.
2. **Fano and Hamming.** The F2 span of the7 incidence vectors is the [7,4,3] binary Hamming code. Exact enumeration gives16 words and weight enumerator `1+7z^3+7z^4+z^7`; its radius-one Hamming balls partition F2^7. This is a real coding theorem. It assumes an intended codeword and at most one bit error to justify correction. Minimum distance3 does not mean every transition has distance3; 0000000 to1111111 has distance7. The code does not prove psychological archetypes, physical attractors, or their uniqueness.
3. **Hexagrams.** F2^6 = F2^3 × F2^3, with six named bit-toggle actions and cube graphQ6. Reversal and complement are explicit automorphisms. Any different64-object set admits a bijection, but preserving its natural relations requires further proof. Codons with one-base substitutions have graphH(3,4), degree9; one-line hexagram changes have graphQ6, degree6. These graphs are not isomorphic despite64 vertices each.
4. **Octonion grading.** In a fixed Cayley–Dickson basis, `e_a e_b = sigma(a,b)e_(a+b)`. The grade product is XOR; sigma is a2-cochain, not a2-cocycle. On a quaternion subalgebra it obeys the cocycle identity; for octonions `(a,b,c)=(1,2,4)` gives left sign+1 and right−1. This distinguishes a graded algebra from a bare Boolean group. See [Baez, The Octonions §2.1](https://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/octonions/node4.html).
5. **Zodiac as a declared cyclic structure.** Twelve ordered positions form Z/12; Chinese remainder gives the group isomorphism Z/12 ≅ Z/4×Z/3. A clockwise step maps to(+1,+1). Relations of equal residue modulo4 and modulo3 give4 classes of3 and3 classes of4 respectively. This preserves a selected cyclic relation, not an unspecified meaning of the elements. G2 has12roots with alternating lengths and Weyl group of order12. The uncoloured twelve-cycle has dihedral automorphism group of order24. Only length-preserving symmetries of the alternating root picture give the order12 subgroup. Its rotational subgroupC6 has no order4 rotation.
6. **Kabbalah needs a graph, not counts.** `symbolica_kabbalah.py:24–66` computes cardinalities3,7,12,22,32. It provides no edge list for a specified Tree-of-Life convention, no map of its10 vertices and22 edges, and no preservation check. A labelled graph on10 nodes22 edges is not Fano's bipartite incidence graph (14nodes21edges) or Fano's collinearity graphK7 (7nodes21edges). A homomorphism, typed relation map, or incidence interpretation remains possible but must be supplied. The3+7+12 partition is a typed set partition, not by itself a graph isomorphism or an octonionic stratification.

## 2. Definite chapter errors and local repairs

- **Line48:** arbitrary R48→Rn maps need not be noninjective for n<48; a continuity hypothesis is essential. Positive definite trace-one states contain a48-dimensional open set, so a continuous injection into Rn, n<48, is impossible (invariance of domain). ## 4. Minimal reusable relation/action contract

Use a many-sorted signature Sigma. A modelA consists of carrier setsA_s, typed relationsR_A⊆A_s1×…×A_sk, observableso:A_s→V_o, and named partial actionsc:D_c⊆A_s→A_t. A correspondence declares:

1. A sort/role assignment and maps h_s:A_s→B_tau(s); names of relations, observation decoders and actions are explicit. Distinguish an axis, an incidence line, an observable, an outcome label and an action.
2. For each claimed relation, preservation `R_A(a) implies R_B(h(a))`. If equivalence is claimed, require reflection as well. Cardinality matching proves none of this.
3. For each retained observable, `o_A = decoder_o ∘ o_B ∘ h`. An injective role map is not needed for a projection but becomes an explicit obligation for a faithful representation.
4. For each executable action, exact definedness on the image `a∈D_c iff h(a)∈D_d`, and commutation `h(c(a))=d(h(a))`. Simulation with implication-only definedness is weaker and must be named accordingly. Preserve any source action equations; then the induced action/path-category map is a functor. A map of nodes alone is insufficient.
5. For a quotientpi:X→Z, every retained observation must be constant on fibers; action domains must be saturated and actions congruent. Otherwise the projected executor is not a function. New observation/action families may require refinement, as in the constructive Autogeny chapters4/6.
6. Empirical interface: a protocol maps raw observations to model data; register uncertainty, calibration, discretization and repeatability separately. A verified finite structural map is not empirical evidence that the chosen tradition instantiates the model.

A useful artifact is therefore a finite relation/action witness with role schemas, source/target encodings, complete map and verification receipt. For F2^3/Fano/Hamming such an artifact is immediately constructible; for a particular Kabbalah graph it requires an actual graph inventory; for cultural/phenomenological readouts it additionally needs a measurement model. This strengthens the project's structural programme without replacing its substantive hypotheses by cardinality arguments.
