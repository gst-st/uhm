# Exact controls for structural-role transport

Reproduce from the UHM checkout with `python3 architecture/symbolica_role_transport.py` (optional `--synarc-root` and `--output`). Detailed exact results, local source hashes and counterexamples are in `architecture/symbolica_role_transport.json`. The new controls are separate from the older I Ching script; production Rust and TeX were not changed. Generic functions use only finite integer positions, relations, graphs and permutations; they contain no traditional symbolic names or answer labels.

The finite hypotheses are confirmed, with an important positive correction: an order-12 **permutation of cross-pair positions** cannot come from a Fano point automorphism, but a genuine order-12 **phase dynamics** can preserve every Fano line projector. These are different action types.

## 1. Seven nonzero binary vectors and the cube are different structures

Take V=F2^3, encoded by integers0,…,7, with vector addition given by XOR. The seven nonzero vectors carry the seven lines {a,b,a XOR b}. The eight vectors carry Q3, with adjacency iff exactly one coordinate changes.

The script checks all7^3 candidate basis images and all7!=5040 permutations of nonzero points. Both constructions give exactly168 line automorphisms, namely GL(3,2). Analytically, a line-preserving map extended by f(0)=0 obeys f(a+b)=f(a)+f(b): the third point on the line through distinct nonzero a,b is unique; equal/zero cases follow separately. An invertible basis can be chosen in7·6·4 ways.

All8!=40320 permutations are checked against cube adjacency. Aut(Q3) has48 elements. Exactly6 fix0, preserve lines, and preserve cube adjacency; these are coordinate permutations. Equivalently, the image of each basis vector must remain a weight-one vector. A specific line-preserving counterexample is

```
f(0,…,7) = (0,1,2,3,5,4,7,6).
```

It sends cube edge(0,4) to(0,5), whose Hamming distance is2. Conversely x↦x XOR1 preserves the cube but moves0, so it is not a linear/Fano identification of the seven nonzero points.

The stronger phrase “coordinate semantics is fixed” must state what is fixed. Preserving the named coordinate-change colours leaves8 translations. Preserving those colours **and** the distinguished0 leaves only the identity. Allowing coordinate relabeling leaves6 origin-preserving maps. These are exact alternatives, not competing interpretations of the same unqualified word “equivalence”.

### Native numbering matters

The local Rust plane is generated by cyclic triples {p,p+1,p+3} using `BY_FANO`, not by XOR on storage indices. The script reads and fingerprints:

* `rust/crates/synarc-math/src/axis.rs:70` — Fano-number-to-storage mapping;
* `rust/crates/synarc-math/src/fano.rs:54` — actual line constructor;
* `rust/crates/synarc-math/src/channels.rs:40` — diagonal line projectors.

All5040 candidate label bridges are checked. Exactly168 identify the native plane with the XOR plane. One explicit storage-index0,…,6→XOR-point bridge is

```
(1,2,4,3,6,5,7).
```

This is the lexicographically first valid bridge, not a semantically distinguished choice. The naive storage-index-plus-one map fails. Named UGM axes impose additional interpretation constraints that incidence alone does not choose.

## 2. Ordered pairs of three-bit positions

An ordered pair (u,v)∈F2^3×F2^3 has64 positions; encode it as u+8v. With one-bit-change adjacency it is Q6. This is the precise binary-coordinate sense in which a six-line configuration is an ordered pair of three-line configurations. No traditional naming, historical ordering, or meaning follows from that cardinality statement.

Aut(Q6) has2^6·6!=46080 maps. Every one of these generated maps is checked against all192 edges. The enumeration is exhaustive by this proof: compose a cube automorphism with a translation to fix0; its six neighbours must permute the basis vectors; after undoing that permutation, every higher-weight vertex is fixed inductively by its lower-weight neighbours and distance from0. The script does **not** enumerate64! permutations.

The block-preserving linear group GL(3,2)×GL(3,2) has28224 maps; every map is checked for Hamming preservation. Exactly36 survive, namely S3×S3. If swapping the two blocks is permitted, the surviving group has72 elements (two disjoint cosets). The full origin-fixed cube group has720 maps and can mix the blocks; swapping bit2 and bit3 is an explicit counterexample to preservation of the ordered-pair decomposition.

The full linear group GL(6,2) is much larger: ∏(64−2^k), k=0,…,5, equals20158709760. It is not enumerated here. The lifted three-bit counterexample already proves that preserving XOR need not preserve Q6 adjacency. Likewise, nonzero translations preserve Q6 but not its additive identity. Thus “same64 states” is not enough to transport both action and composition semantics.

## 3. A twelve-position cycle and two modular roles

For n∈Z12, the map

```
n -> (n mod3, n mod4)
(a,b) -> (4a + 9b) mod12
```

is a bijection and a ring isomorphism. The script checks every144 addition pair and every144 multiplication pair, the inverse on all12 values, and the complete clock orbit.

One step around the directed cycle becomes `(a,b) -> (a+1 mod3,b+1 mod4)`. It is not one-coordinate-at-a-time motion in the Cartesian product C3□C4: the undirected cycle has degree2 while that product has degree4. The coordinate residues can represent a declared repeating three-class role and four-class role, including a chosen modality/element schedule. The labels, starting point, orientation and empirical interpretation are additional premises; CRT proves none of their purported causal powers.

## 4. A Fano line, its complement, and twelve cross-pairs

Choose line L={1,2,3} and complement C={4,5,6,7}. There are12 ordered cross-pairs L×C. The script also repeats the stabilizer-order check for all seven lines, so the result does not depend on the selected line.

The line stabilizer in GL(3,2) has24 elements. Its action on C gives all24 permutations of four points faithfully, hence it is S4. A direct geometric reason for faithfulness: each point of L labels one of the three perfect matchings of the four complementary points, through the sums of the matched pairs. Once the complement action is known, the line action is fixed.

The exact element-order distribution is1:1, 2:9, 3:8, 4:6. There is no order12. The full Fano group also has no order12: its orders are1:1, 2:21, 3:56, 4:42, 7:48. Therefore no point automorphism induces a12-cycle on the cross-pairs. The stabilizer is nevertheless transitive on those12 pairs: transitivity of a group is not the existence of one cyclic generator acting transitively.

In contrast, the partition-preserving group S3×S4 has144 elements, including12 of order12. All12 fail the Fano-line test. A concrete example is `(1 2 3)(4 5 6 7)`. It cycles the cross-pairs in this order:

```
(1,4),(2,5),(3,6),(1,7),(2,4),(3,5),
(1,6),(2,7),(3,4),(1,5),(2,6),(3,7).
```

But it sends Fano line{1,4,5} to{2,5,6}, which is not a Fano line. Thus the exact clock transport exists after forgetting some incidence constraints; it is not simultaneously a transport of the complete incidence structure under that point-permutation action.

In Hilbert-space terms, a selected line gives C7=C3⊕C4, and its oriented cross-block operators form Hom(C4,C3), of complex dimension12. This does not turn C7 into C3⊗C4 or a twelve-dimensional state space. Hermitian conjugacy pairs the two cross-blocks, and density positivity couples their entries. A correspondence among12 cross-pair positions should declare this operator-level type explicitly.

## 5. Positive correction: geometry-preserving twelve-phase dynamics

Let P_L be the diagonal projector onto the chosen three-point line, and ζ a primitive twelfth root of unity. Define

```
U = ζ^4 P_L + ζ^3 (I-P_L).
Γ -> U Γ U*.
```

This is a unitary channel. It preserves all seven diagonal Fano line projectors pointwise, since they commute with U. Within-block coherences stay unchanged; each oriented L→C coherence is multiplied by ζ^(4−3)=ζ. Thus the induced phase action has exact period12 whenever such a coherence is nonzero. The script verifies all144 phase-composition cases using integer exponents modulo12, all12 distinct actions, and all diagonal-projector preservation premises without floating-point approximations.

For an explicit valid state family, choose a∈L,b∈C:

```
Γ_k = I7/7 + (ζ^k E_ab + ζ^(-k) E_ba)/14.
```

The eigenvalues are3/14,1/14 and five copies of1/7, independent of k. They are positive and sum to1. Conjugation by U takes Γ_k toΓ_(k+1), giving twelve distinct states. This is a clock in cross-block **phase**, not a cyclic permutation of the twelve cross-pair **positions**.

The clock needs an observation signature. All diagonal-projector expectations and all closed phase products W_C remain unchanged along this orbit. In a fixed complex frame, the two observables X_ab=E_ab+E_ba and Y_ab=i(E_ab−E_ba) have expectations cos(2πk/12)/7 and sin(2πk/12)/7; together they distinguish all twelve states. If arbitrary diagonal rephasings are instead identified as gauge, the entire orbit represents one gauge-equivalence class. No observable clock is established for a signature containing only diagonal projectors or rephasing-invariant quantities.

A stronger exact characterization follows. The unitary normalizer of the set of seven diagonal Fano line projectors is `U(1)^7 ⋊ GL(3,2)`. Indeed, the product of two different line projectors is the rank-one point projector at their intersection; all seven point projectors occur (all21 pair intersections are checked). Hence those line projectors generate the entire diagonal algebra. Any unitary permuting the line projectors normalizes this algebra and must be monomial: a diagonal phase matrix followed by a coordinate permutation. That permutation must preserve the Fano lines, while the phases are arbitrary. Conversely every such monomial unitary works. Modulo the globally ineffective phase, the group is `U(1)^6 ⋊ GL(3,2)`.

The normalizer is strictly for the **set of seven diagonal Fano line projectors**. It is not asserted to preserve an oriented three-form, octonion multiplication, or the complete UHM dynamics.

This normalizer statement is an analytic proof using the exhaustively checked finite incidence premise, not an enumeration of a continuous unitary group. It explains precisely why the finite permutation obstruction does not prohibit compatible temporal dynamics. The native `write_pair_phased`/`pair_phase` APIs already distinguish complex phase (`synarc-core/src/holon.rs:595,615`); this audit does not claim that the above symbolic clock bridge is already wired into them.

## 6. Consequence for a generic role bridge

Represent a source by its positions, named relations, permitted actions, observations and fixed labels. A bridge declares which of these structures it preserves and records an explicit checked map. The controls above support several legitimate transports, with different obligations:

* XOR/Fano incidence permits168 basis changes; adding cube adjacency permits6; fixing coordinate roles and the origin permits1.
* CRT transports the clock through simultaneous modular increments; it does not transport Cartesian-product adjacency.
* The twelve cross-pair positions support a clock under a partition permutation, but that action changes Fano incidence.
* A twelve-phase unitary clock preserves the Fano projectors, while acting on a different observable than pair position.

Therefore structural position can transfer rigorously without either rejecting meaningful interpretations or treating numerical coincidences as proofs of them. The implementation contract should keep position permutations, phase channels, coordinate labels, and empirical semantic witnesses as distinct typed data. It should require the appropriate observation/action commuting diagram for the chosen action type. No result here alone establishes consciousness, a physical astrology, a canonical assignment of spiritual meanings, or new benchmark capability.
