# Remaining SYNARC phase-support obligation

Source snapshot: SYNARC main `41a57f5ace48d7307b16f9c134e9277ce535cbc8`, inspected 2026-10-13. The accompanying JSON fingerprints the actual source files. This is a verified mathematical counterexample and source audit, **not** a completed SYNARC repair or a native Rust execution receipt.

`fano::holonomy` and `off_plane_holonomy` return angle zero when a triangle product has zero modulus. `holonomy::phase_passport` combines seven Fano triangles and eight other triangles into fifteen angle/carrier pairs. The carrier explicitly records absence, so a zero angle there should not be read as a measured flat phase. More fundamentally, even retaining every triangle cannot describe all phase data on a sparse support.

Take Gamma=M/28, where M has diagonal4, M01=M12=M23=1, M30=i and the Hermitian conjugates. All other off-diagonal entries vanish. Trace is one; every Gershgorin left endpoint is at least1/14, so the state is positive definite. Its support is a square plus three isolated vertices: m=4,n=7,c=4, hence one independent cycle. Every one of its35 triangle products is zero, but the square product is i/28^4 and its holonomy is pi/2. The checked Gamma constructor retains valid matrices unchanged, so sparsity is an admissible state, not an impossible input. The existing fixed fifteen-triangle passport consequently reports fifteen zero carriers while missing this nonzero supported cycle.

A positive exact control confirms the dense construction: expressing its seven oriented Fano triangles and eight OFF_PLANE triangles in the integral star-at-zero cycle basis gives determinant1 over the rationals. Thus the selected cycles form a genuine integer basis on full K7, not merely a real-linear spanning family; there is no additional finite lattice ambiguity in this dense passport. The support counterexample does not invalidate that result.

This does not mean SYNARC ignores phase altogether: `Holon::pair_phase` already returns `Option<f64>` with its explicit signal threshold, and the dense phase passport includes eight non-Fano triangles. The unmet obligation is a complete support-aware phase representation, plus clear separation of an absent angle, a measured zero angle, and the signal-strength policy. Product-underflow must also not define support: sum edge arguments after checking individual support.

The Homoholograph repair in this work supplies the mathematical pattern: spanning forest, m−n+c chord coordinates, cycle reconstruction modulo2pi, explicit missingness and calibration valid only on its declared support. Integration into SYNARC still requires its own native API migration, specification alignment, tests of acquisition/execution consumers and normal conveyor validation. Copying a human-facing interpretation into SYNARC would not discharge these obligations. No benchmark improvement is claimed by this audit.
