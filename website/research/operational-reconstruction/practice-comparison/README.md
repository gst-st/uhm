# Can a contemplative report distinguish coherence dynamics from ordinary memory?

This is a prospective protocol. No participant has been enrolled and no human effect is estimated here. The [previous study](../practice-study/README.md) predicts a parity advantage under a specified report channel. An ordinary one-bit counter suffices for that prediction. The operational canon specifies preparations, actions and readouts. To obtain an additional behavioural prediction one must say which experience prepares which state, what a question does to it, and what information survives until the next answer. This protocol fixes two such hypotheses and their conventional competitors before seeing outcomes. The two experiments use separate cohorts and answer different questions. ## Reports about one event

Randomly assign 2,048 consenting adults per arm to silent exhalation counting (1 to 10, restart) or following the felt airflow without counting. Use the same posture, nasal breathing without pacing, room, and two-minute instruction and settling period. Participants may pause or withdraw. Neither instruction asks participants to change their breathing depth. Four blocks of 18 trials are completed over two visits, blocks 1–2 then 3–4, with a break between blocks. Before each trial allow three unprobed breaths. Use the next completed exhalation, marked by the same neutral cue at its end. Keep the synced respiratory trace and controller output. A landmark identifies a shared interval of physical time; it does not identify felt attention.

The question is “Did you clearly feel the air at your nostrils during the [first / middle / last] third of that exhalation?” The three substitutions are A, B and C. Participants have the same yes/no buttons in an assigned counterbalanced arrangement throughout. A trial records agreement of the two endpoint answers. Both “no” and “yes” are included. No part of the analysis selects breaths on which the first answer was “yes”.

Each block has two of each direct pair AB, BA, BC, CB, AC, CA and one each of AA, BB, CC, ABC, A-C and A0C, in concealed random order. Reveal each query at its scheduled instant. On a direct or repeated pair show the queries 0.5 and 3.0 seconds after the marked endpoint. The three-slot conditions use 0.5, 3.0 and 5.5 seconds. At the middle slot ABC asks B, A-C asks “Is the displayed dot filled?” with an independent random dot and the same two buttons, and A0C is silent. Define a two-second response window from each actual prompt onset, taking the first completed key press. Keep the clocks running if a response is missing. Follow the ordinary breath instruction between questions. Match visual, auditory and motor demands where the intervention permits them. No accuracy feedback is given. Different end delays make direct AC and A0C distinct measurements.

Let a, b and z be the direct agreement rates for AB/BA, BC/CB and AC/CA. The prepared two-mode model uses a fixed pair of complementary rank-one projections per question. Both signs reset the sector. There is a stable mapping of answer labels across questions and negligible or equalized evolution between paired probes. Normalized question directions u,v,w on the Bloch sphere give adjacent agreement (1+u·v)/2, etc. Expanding the nonnegative square ‖5u−8v+5w‖² yields **25z−40a−40b+56 ≥ 0**. It holds under mixtures with common weights. When both adjacent rates are at least .90 the bound is .64, and a triple of rays attains it. Three arbitrary temporal descriptions do not prescribe projective measurements. This geometry and its stronger nonlinear overlap bounds have precedents. Hide AC, CA and the three-slot outcomes from calibration. For each person convert the adjacent agreement rates to signed scores x=2a−1 and y=2b−1. Form u=(x_odd y_even+x_even y_odd)/2 using blocks 1,3 and 2,4. Under conditional independence of the two halves and stable individual parameters, the expected turn and serial predictions are respectively the participant means of u and (1+u)/2. The first point prediction additionally requires equal adjacent, same-signed turns. A half-and-half mixture of people with s=.80 and s=1 yields .68 versus .84, despite a pooled neighbour mean of .90. Directed rates, exact repeated queries, between-half shifts and unequal adjacent means are diagnostics for these assumptions. They cannot establish independence of latent drift. The exact reset model also requires perfectly repeatable measurements with effectively no intervening evolution or button noise. A single verified different answer on AA, BB or CC contradicts this exact person-to-instrument hypothesis. The scorer exposes this gate even when the AC residual is small. With no observed repeats it reports the gate as unobserved. A systematic violation of another necessary condition undermines attribution even with a small residual. Specify any alternative dynamics in a new study.

Analyse participants with equal weight within their randomized arm; the two arms need not have equal calibrations. Neither hypothesis specifies which practice should win. The six primary contrasts per arm are z−u, z−(1+u)/2, z+1−a−b, (25z−40a−40b+56)/25, agreement(ABC)−agreement(A-C) and agreement(A-C)−agreement(A0C). For the first two reject the equality if the simultaneous interval excludes zero. For the next two reject only for an interval wholly below zero. The last two estimate intervention and motor/timing control effects. No omnibus choice of a winning school. Use two-sided Hoeffding intervals at fixed N, with Bonferroni correction for all 12 contrasts. Each independent sampling unit is one person. The local intervals contain every allowed completion of every missing endpoint, including an entirely absent participant. Rejection of either, both, or neither point model is possible. ## Eight messages during contemplation

In a separate cohort randomize 1,024 adults per arm to the same practice instructions. Give 24 trials per person with breaks after trials 8 and 16. Present three 100 ms low/high tones (440/880 Hz, equalized loudness) at three successive exhalation endpoints. A fresh uniformly selected three-bit word determines the tones, in time order, on every trial. Repetition is allowed both within and between trials. Use identical masking noise for 500 ms after the last tone, then the same 2-second silent delay before the query. This timing can reduce sensory carry-over. It cannot certify that it vanished. After the delay reveal either all eight response options (one choice) or a single cued position (one low/high choice), for five seconds. Use the assigned button orientation or reversed grid, decode choices to positions in chronological order, keep time-outs as unknown, record actual stimuli and cue, give no feedback. Participants practice the interface beforehand with disjoint trials; hearing and equipment checks are completed before allocation. Keep the upcoming target and retrieval cue inaccessible to the participant and the session operator. Record the delivered sound via loopback. Exclude notes, phones, external prompts and displayed previous tones. A participant's internal rehearsal is a possible extra carrier that the experiment itself cannot eliminate.

The complete accessible trace hypothesis H7 says that all information about the fresh message at the final choice resides in a single normalized positive state ρ on seven complex dimensions. One common, target-independent whole-word measurement has eight positive effects Eᵢ summing to I. Shared randomness, including prior history, may enter both devices if it is independent of the fresh target. Extra target-dependent side information, concurrent independent carriers, or an assisting correlated quantum register invalidate this closure. Since Tr(ρᵢEᵢ)≤Tr(Eᵢ), the average correct whole-word identification is **at most 7/8**. Seven orthogonal encodings and a duplicate attain the limit. This known dimension bound is also attained by seven classical states; it is the proposed connection to recall in the specified practical task that is being tested. [Seven-readout derivation](../network/SevenReadout.lean) · [Frenkel and Weiner](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-015-2463-0)

The ordinary three-bit rival recovers one joint word from the same retained record and projects the requested bit from that word. It gives **z≥p₁+p₂+p₃−2** because a wrong word must contain at least one wrong bit. Independent bit errors are unnecessary. Thus at .98 accuracy on each bit the whole-word prediction is ≥.94. Eight perfectly distinguishable classical preparations reach 1. Query-specific reconstruction, a longer search on single-bit requests, and a change in strategy caused by the cue can all violate the common-decoder premise. Reliable z>.875 questions the single-register closure for this preparation; scores below .875 also occur with ordinary forgetting. Neither result alone measures the dimensionality of subjective experience.

Generate the cue independently with replacement on **every** trial, P(all)=1/2 and P(each bit)=1/6. Cue and target use separate random draws. There are no within-person quotas or rerandomization for imbalance. Use centered inverse-probability scores. With L_w the number of errors on whole-word trials and L_j errors on single-bit j trials, the assigned-person scores are Z=1−2L_w/24 and Pⱼ=1−6Lⱼ/24. Their expectations are the accuracies that would be observed under the corresponding requests. Errors and encoding may vary with previous trials. The cue must remain independent of the current pre-query record. A participant's Z and Pⱼ can fall outside the unit interval. Retaining those values preserves the estimator. The slack S=Z+2−ΣPⱼ equals (6ΣLⱼ−2L_w)/24. If no trial of one kind was drawn, its error count is zero in this estimator. Do not impute successful recalls or interpret that individual's estimate as a measured proportion.

At fixed N the one-sided nulls are E[Z]≤7/8 and E[S]≥0. Z ranges over [−1,1] and S over [−2,6]. For each null use a one-sided Hoeffding bound and one preregisterable product statistic. The factors are 1+(Z−7/8)/2 and 1−S/8. All are strictly positive. For independent people under the corresponding average null, the expectation of the product is at most one: independence gives the product of expectations, and AM–GM bounds that product by their mean raised to N. Markov's inequality gives a fixed-horizon test. Use the least favourable completion of missing data for each factor. Across two arms, two directions and two methods the family error is at most .05, using .05/8 per test and product threshold 160. No interim testing. Report both tests and all four scheduled/observed cue counts; declare an exclusion only when the relevant responses were actually sampled. Report neither, either or both exclusions. The two experiments have separate inference families; a post-hoc global discovery claim is outside this design. ## Collection, freeze and replay

Before a human run obtain study review and consent, settle hearing/screen/response accessibility, trial timing and hardware qualification on disjoint feasibility participants, and register the dated protocol, recruitment and stopping rule externally. Never use feasibility outcomes to choose a sign, sector, delay, threshold or success story in the confirmatory cohort. If equipment or the study design changes, version and register the amended protocol using a fresh cohort. Recruit independent individuals. Site-wide coaching, related participants or sessions run in groups would require a different unit of analysis.

1. Enrol the fixed roster before allocation. Provide a CSV headed `participant` with one pseudonym per line. Use a new 32-byte cryptographically random secret per study, supplied by an independent allocator. In private storage run `python3 -B protocol.py freeze order ids.csv secret.bin run-order` or choose `capacity` and a different destination. Publish the printed SHA-256 with a timestamp **before sessions**. Escrow the secret, schedule, roster and source revision separately from the session and analysis teams. Predetermined pseudorandom draws implement the ideal independent allocation; predictability or disclosure of the schedule invalidates that assumption. The same inputs reproduce the assignment.
2. The apparatus releases only the current scheduled prompt. Archive actual stimulus, timing, respiratory and button records before looking at outcomes. Use the breath landmark extractor validated on an independent equipment session. Define invalid delivery as wrong cue, missing or wrong breath, missing actual stimulus evidence, order mismatch or deviation from scheduled onset by more than 100 ms. Score early/late or absent keys as missing. Capture any displayed middle question and any answer to it even when the endpoint score uses only the other two answers. A missed middle answer does not convert an active prompt to silence. Keep eligibility, tolerances and raw logs fixed and available for independent audit. The files here allocate and score; device acquisition, independent verification of raw traces and ethical approval belong to a real collection site.
3. Normalize button responses using the assigned physical mapping. The order CSV header is `participant,block,trial,delivered,first,last`. The endpoints are 0/1 or blank. The capacity header is `participant,trial,delivered_target,delivered_cue,answer`; words are three binary characters, including leading zeros, and bit1 is the first presented. Supply the actual delivered condition from the acquisition log. Use blank delivery for an unverified or mistimed trial. Unscheduled records and duplicate trials fail validation. Missing assigned slots stay in their assigned denominators. Archive raw logs and the deterministic device-specific conversion before unblinding and have an independent custodian timestamp the normalized CSV digest and raw archive digest.
4. Run `python3 -B protocol.py analyze run-order PRIOR_SHA observations.csv OUTCOME_SHA` from the frozen checkout, or supply the capacity directory. It verifies the schedule, roster, code, protocol and observation byte hashes. Local hashes alone establish integrity; external timestamps and custody establish precedence. The lower-level scorers also print their fixed sample sizes, score ranges and radii with `--plan`. Simulated control cases live in the unit tests. No human is represented by them.
5. Release arm-level observed counts, completion bounds, both successful and unsuccessful contrasts, and protocol deviations. Preserve the distinction between physical delivery, first-person judgement and a model of that judgement. Independent replication is needed for a claim about a contemplative population.
