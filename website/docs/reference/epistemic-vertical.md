# The Epistemic Vertical

The revision of 2026-10-03 withdraws the former verdict that all structural gaps are closed. The [mathematical kernel](/docs/reference/mathematical-kernel) and [premises](/docs/reference/premises) specify the current scopes. Coinciding numbers and finite machine checks do not transfer a proof between levels.

## Three application domains {#этажи}

| Domain | Correct object | Additional input |
|---|---|---|
| Universe and physics | A specified site, sheaves, chosen fields and joint-state spaces | Physical identification, scale, matter module, preparation, and clocks |
| Subject and dynamics | Augmented state record, feedback family, capability gates | Experiential extension, observation law, and phenomenal interpretation |
| Foundations of mathematics | An explicit algebra, grading, code, or doctrine with stated axioms | A comparison map and verification of those axioms for each new object |

No row derives every other row. Agreement of Fano geometry in two constructions establishes a common finite object only after an explicit isomorphism; it does not establish identical physical causes or cognitive levels.

## Transfer obligations {#критерии}

A transfer requires:

1. Typed domains, codomains, and a transfer map.
2. Verification of the source theorem's hypotheses.
3. Preservation of the structure and observables actually used.
4. A distinction between isomorphism, embedding, reduction, and selected aggregation.
5. Independent verification of the physical or phenomenal bridge.

The joint space of two seven-axis holons has dimension 49; a seven-dimensional aggregate discards information. A higher hierarchy is not determined by a finite matrix or attenuation factor. Perfect single-error binary diagnosis gives a conditional bound $N\ge7$, permits length 15, and does not imply a normed division algebra.

## Open obligations {#регистр-дыр}

| Obligation | Current scope |
|---|---|
| Physical selection of seven axes | [P/H]; exact code and representation bounds require their own hypotheses |
| Reconstruction uniqueness | [H] strong comparison (RI); measurement requires observation-law identifiability |
| Terminal self-model of the Universe | Requires a carrier, clock extension, and dynamics; the terminal sheaf selects no numerical anchor |
| Universal depth-three ceiling | Withdrawn; a selected SAD score and coding ladder do not bound general cognitive depth |
| Natural realization of a Fano doctrine | Proofs inside a chosen octonionic construction are relative to that construction; independent realization is tested separately |
| Experience from structure | [P/I]; a numerical criterion does not derive phenomenal identification |
| Physical spacetime and matter | [H/I] module, field, and clock bridges; finite algebraic computations do not replace them |

## Numerical agreements {#резонансы}

An agreement of seven, attenuation $1/3$, a score ceiling, or a group dimension becomes structural only through an explicit map of derivations. Twenty-one complex matrix pairs are not automatically twenty-one independent addresses, $\dim G_2=14$ is not automatically fourteen control degrees of freedom, and two values of three do not identify their hierarchies.

## Independent inputs {#края}

The assertion that exactly two inputs remain and everything else is a theorem is withdrawn. Beyond the quantum formalism and phenomenal identification, the site and metric, dimension and frame, dynamical family, controls and preparation, clocks, experiential extension and readout, observation law, and physical module bridges are specified. Independence or equivalence requires a model or proof; sentence count does not measure independent assumptions.

## Consistency discipline {#дисциплина}

Each result has one current status and an explicit source. A historical closure verdict is not a proof premise. Changed assumptions propagate to dependent pages in both locales. Status checks, site builds, and numerical witnesses verify their stated properties; they are not formal verification of the whole corpus.

General audit promises are replaced by checkable obligations. The [status registry](/docs/reference/status-registry) records current results, and the [identifiability protocol](/docs/applied/research/reconstruction-identifiability) states when measurements can test them independently.
