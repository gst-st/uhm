# Status of results

Numbers identify specific claims and their assumptions. A theorem about an assigned representation, action, instrument or encoder applies to those data. A physical or phenomenal identification has its own evidential status. A result marked **[✗]** cannot serve as a premise. [Mathematical kernel](./mathematical-kernel) · [Premises](./premises) · [Verified network results](./network-results).

## Status markers {#стратификация-строгости}

- **[T]** Theorem — strictly proven
- **[C]** Conditional theorem — proven under an explicitly stated assumption
- **[H]** Hypothesis — an unestablished claim; mathematical claims require proof, empirical claims require operationalisation and testing
- **[P]** Postulate — accepted without proof as a fundamental assumption
- **[Pr]** Research programme — a named open direction, neither assumed nor claimed proven
- **[D]** Definition — definition by convention (assigned, not derived)
- **[I]** Interpretation — philosophical/semantic statement
- **[✗]** Retracted — proven erroneous or withdrawn
