# Frontier Ledger: UHM vs the Field

:::info Why this page
A mature theory must compete where others already predict. UHM's structural prohibitions (T-296 no second doublet, T-297 no gauge $Z'$, $N_{gen}=3$, FE-uniqueness without leptoquarks) are not silence — at every live frontier they force a *definite* prediction, usually the boldest one on the table: **exact Standard-Model emptiness** where competitors expect signals, and definite *positive* signals elsewhere (O-sector relic, axion, proton decay, Majorana $0\nu\beta\beta$). Each row is falsifiable; several have already been graded by experiment.
:::

| # | Frontier | Competitors predict | **UHM predicts** | Status |
|---|----------|--------------------|------------------|--------|
| 1 | Muon $g{-}2$ | SUSY/2HDM/$Z'$/leptoquark contributions explain the (g−2) anomaly | **Zero BSM contribution** [T ← T-296,297]: experiment must converge to the *full lattice* SM value | Lattice HVP (BMW-class) already drifts toward SM ✓ open |
| 2 | LFV: $\mu\to e\gamma$, $\mu\to 3e$, $\mu N\to eN$ | Many BSM: rates near current bounds | **Neutrino-loop floor only** ($\sim10^{-54}$): MEG-II, Mu3e, Mu2e see *nothing* [T ← no new bosons/scalars] | open (any signal falsifies) |
| 3 | Lepton universality $R_K, R_{K^*}$ | Leptoquark/$Z'$ models predicted deviations | **Exact universality** [T ← FE without X,Y; T-297] | LHCb 2022 returned to SM ✓ *post-factum pass* |
| 4 | Electron EDM | SUSY/2HDM: $10^{-29}$–$10^{-30}\,e\cdot$cm | **EDM desert**: no new CP phases without a second doublet ⇒ $d_e\sim$ SM $10^{-38}$ [T-consequence + [C] phase-completeness] | ACME/JILA bound $10^{-30}$ — desert holds ✓ open |

**Reading the ledger.** Rows 1–4 and 10–12 are *prohibition-driven*: they follow from the same three structural theorems, so one confirmed BSM discovery in any of them breaks the whole block — UHM is maximally exposed, which is what falsifiability means. Rows 5–9 are *positive* UHM numbers with named experiments. Post-factum passes (row 3; the deflation trends in 1 and 10) are graded honestly: predictions written after the data count only as consistency, never as discovery.
