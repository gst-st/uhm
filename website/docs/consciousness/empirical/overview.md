# The Empirical Programme: Four Tasks

The programme separates proofs within the declared matrix/category model from observations linking that model to a substrate and to reported experience. A model can be mathematically exact and empirically misidentified. The [typed kernel](/docs/reference/mathematical-kernel) and [reconstruction-identifiability conditions](/docs/applied/research/reconstruction-identifiability) define the relevant boundaries.

## Starting point {#starting-point}

Two-aspect monism is the interpretation **[I]** that physical and experiential descriptions refer to one underlying structure. It is not derived by existence of a topos, a density matrix or an adjunction.

The surviving mathematics includes HS purity identities, specified channels, partial-trace uniqueness **after a tensor factorisation is supplied**, spectral projectors of a normalised state, and Yoneda within a declared category. It does not establish a universal $\mathrm{Coh}_E>1/7$ floor, a unique numerical/phenomenal functor, forced experiential content, or an impossibility of internal semantic maps. The former T-38a floor and T-214 universal no-go are withdrawn; [T-213](/docs/proofs/categorical/fundamental-closures#t-213) also does not identify a channel output with a representable sheaf or give a universal 138-bit description bound.

The remaining work is to identify the observation map, test independently labelled tasks and evaluate the experiential interpretation. Logical support-reflector uniqueness applies to its fixed slice and inclusion; it selects no unique numerical state model.

## Four tasks {#four-tasks}

| Task | Question | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration | Which observations identify which state/model features? | Declared observation equations, identifiability, training/held-out data, uncertainty |
| Structure | Does a proposed geometry predict independent relations between reports? | Measured similarity/discrimination matrices and model comparisons |
| Mechanism | Can specified dynamics be implemented and its task bridge survive ablations? | State-preserving implementations plus independent functional tests |
| Phenomenal interpretation | Why identify the modelled structure with experience? | Explicit philosophical/empirical bridge, separated from the proved identities |

### Calibration {#task-calibration}

Specify $\pi_{\mathrm{bio}}$ or an artificial-substrate estimator, data distribution, reference frame, observables and latent equivalence classes. PSD and unit trace are admissibility conditions, not evidence of injectivity. Diagonals, magnitudes and pairwise absolute phase gaps can leave inequivalent states observationally indistinguishable. Fit with independently known targets; validate held-out predictions and report nonidentifiability rather than choosing a latent state silently.

A proposed quality space may use rays and the Fubini–Study metric, but neither the form of the experiential lift nor the assignment of a ray to “red” follows uniquely from CPTP compatibility. Semantic calibration must be tested independently. See [Calibration](./calibration).

### Structure {#task-structure}

A stipulated relational geometry has testable content once its metric, observations and admissible transformations are fixed. For a genuine metric space, the complete distance profile identifies a point; finite probes give controlled approximation only under stated covering hypotheses. This result does not prove that phenomenal qualities have no other properties, nor that the selected geometry is physically correct. Compare competing geometries before interpreting a successful fit. See [Structure](./structure) and [the categorical formalism](/docs/proofs/categorical/categorical-formalism#enriched-yoneda).

Canonical $\Phi=(P-Q)/Q$ depends on the matrix and frame, not on the site's topology $J$. Refining $J$ alone cannot force new coherence pairs or change that $\Phi$. A separately declared score summing over nested pair sets is nondecreasing because its summands are nonnegative **[T]**; this arithmetic must not be mistaken for a theorem linking topology refinement to experience. See [T-210](/docs/proofs/categorical/fundamental-closures#t-210).

### Mechanism {#task-engineering}

Specify channels or the nonlinear vector field, numerical model $M$, rates, gate, source terms and stability hypotheses. Fixed-input CPTP structure is different from a full state-dependent map. E-ablation, monitoring and self-reports are model/substrate hypotheses, not universal necessities from a scalar window. Test them with valid interventions, control ablations and independent targets. See [Engineering](./engineering).

### The hard problem and the bridge {#hard-problem}

A successful functional or geometric test corroborates its model at its tested scope. Calling the represented structure phenomenal is an additional interpretation **[I/H]**. This programme does not offer a mathematical solution to why structure is felt; it also proves no general impossibility of studying or internally representing that correspondence.

Lawvere's theorem concerns a weakly point-surjective evaluator $A\to B^A$ and fixed points of endomorphisms of $B$. Its assumptions do not hold for every internal map from states to reports or experience. The alleged T-214 prohibition is therefore withdrawn. A chosen noise channel may erase particular phase information; this does not make all physical observations insensitive to phases or decide a philosophical no-go.

## Scope of evidence {#non-claims}

Protocols proposed here have status **[Pr/H]** unless an explicit mathematical theorem is stated with its assumptions. A simulation of a formula checks code/model consistency; it is not independent evidence of a biological interpretation. A reconstructed threshold trained against the same behavioural threshold cannot validate that threshold. There is no universal clinical diagnosis, cognitive-depth ceiling or no-zombie conclusion from a static scalar.
