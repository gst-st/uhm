Historical testimony can suggest **which distinctions to look for**. A practice, the recognition of an event and its report are three different operations. Record the actual instruction, the practitioner's words in the original language, timing, interruptions and the meaning of success within that practice.

MN 118 explicitly includes discerning long and short breaths. The Sōtō instruction for zazen includes quiet natural breathing. Ignatius's particular examen calls for retrospective review of a selected fault and recording counts at specified times. These sources give different targets and times of reporting. A common vocabulary for inner states would require an additional comparative study. ## A discriminating experiment {#experiment}

First agree with practitioners on **one** event both instructions allow them to identify. An example for a separate attention task is noticing a lapse and returning to the chosen activity. An immediate button press has been used in a focused-attention experiment. The timestamp measures the report of recognition. The lapse itself can start earlier. [Hasenkamp et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21782031/)

[H] Compare two practice conditions and two independently assigned report modes, for example an immediate mark and subsequent placement on a session timeline. Define the common event, start cue, clock, unit and handling of missing or ambiguous marks in advance. Randomize or counterbalance session order within people. Do not replace an absent report by zero latency. Preserve accounts that contradict the proposed correspondence. A source describing a different aim can enter the archive without being pooled into this experiment.

[T] For an exact table of times $T(s,m)$, an additive model $a(s)+b(m)$ exists precisely when, at a fixed reference condition $r$ and mode $c$,
$$
T(s,m)+T(r,c)=T(s,c)+T(r,m)
$$
for every tested cell. The mixed contrast tests whether a change of reporting mode has the same effect across practice conditions. Even if it vanishes, adding a constant to all $a$ and subtracting it from all $b$ produces the same records. Absolute inner onset needs an independent anchor and a validated observation model.

In the finite positive control the rows are $(1,5)$ and $(2,6)$. A competitor produces $(1,5)$ and $(2,7)$, changing the contrast by one tick. These are mathematical witnesses. In a human study predefine precision, repeats and a held-out replication, and estimate the interaction with uncertainty. Agreement would support transfer of the specified report statistic. A claim about a shared experience also requires the original accounts and a separate discriminating intervention.
