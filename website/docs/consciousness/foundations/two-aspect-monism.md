# Two-Aspect Monism and Its Mathematical Scope

UHM proposes that physical and experiential descriptions refer to aspects of one underlying structure **[I]**. This is an ontological interpretation. The formal model supplies states, processes and candidate content records; identifying such a record with experience requires an additional bridge. Neither an adjunction nor a purity cutoff proves that identification.

A seven-dimensional density matrix is the selected numerical state model. It is not itself a sheaf, a terminal object, a tensor product of seven subsystems, or a complete theory of every conscious phenomenon. The [typed kernel](/docs/reference/mathematical-kernel) fixes these distinctions.

## Precedents and related programmes {#прецеденты-и-родственные-программы}

Two-aspect interpretations and relational approaches to experience predate UHM. Their relevance is conceptual; an analogy with an earlier philosophy does not prove a channel property, a universal phase gap or a phenomenal bridge.

### Pauli and Jung {#паули-юнг}

The Pauli–Jung programme treats mental and physical descriptions as complementary aspects of a neutral domain. Relevant developments include Atmanspacher, *Dual-aspect monism à la Pauli and Jung* (2012), and [the formally augmented outline (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1515/opphil-2020-0138). UHM's numerical register is a different model choice; its E label does not automatically supply a tensor factor or a canonical mental/physical split.

### Bohm {#бом-активная-информация}

Bohm's active-information programme motivates comparisons between physical organisation and experience. The analogy with a state-dependent regeneration coefficient is **[I]**. It does not establish a universal lower E-coherence floor. The old T-38a claim $\mathrm{Viable}\Rightarrow\mathrm{Coh}_E>1/7$ is withdrawn in the bootstrap/source-dependent dynamics; [its corrected scope](/docs/applied/coherence-cybernetics/theorems#теорема-81-условная-необходимость-интериорности-no-zombie) requires additional assumptions for any dynamical bound.

### Velmans {#велманс-рефлексивный-монизм}

Reflexive monism treats first- and third-person descriptions as perspectives on a common reality. UHM's parallel is an interpretation, not a deduction that perspective differences are uniquely realised by one matrix extraction.

### Chalmers {#чалмерс-двуаспектная-информация}

Double-aspect information and organisational invariance motivate questions about structure and substrate dependence. A declared UHM capability criterion is operational **[D/Pr]**; it does not prove a unique phenomenal functor or assign an observed level to a thermostat by purity alone.

### Russellian monism {#расселианский-монизм-поле}

A relational mathematical content model describes structure. Identifying all experiential character with that structure is an extra philosophical thesis **[I]**. Yoneda identifies objects up to isomorphism in a selected category; it cannot establish that phenomenality has no properties outside that representation.

### Tsuchiya and Saigo {#цучия-сайго}

Category-theoretic approaches explicitly preceded UHM: [Tsuchiya, Taguchi and Saigo (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neures.2015.12.007), [Tsuchiya and Saigo (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niab034), and [Tsuchiya, Phillips and Saigo (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.concog.2022.103319). Their work motivates testing relational models and categorical correspondences. UHM adds specified matrix/metric proposals; the earlier universal uniqueness and faithfulness assertions on this page do not survive the typed audit.

### What remains after comparison {#итог-прецедентов}

The philosophical identity is not novel merely because it is written categorically. The mathematically assessable additions are explicitly constructed state/process models, conditional identities and independently testable bridges. No-zombie necessity, a forced experiential split and universal semantic no-go results cannot be used as established advantages.

## Categorical formulation {#категориальная-формализация}

Use the Bures open site $\operatorname{Open}(D_7)$ and its sheaf $\infty$-topos. A numerical state is a point of $D_7$; a chosen sheaf/object $G$ is different data. The support reflector $\operatorname{im}_G\dashv j$ is unique up to the usual equivalence for its **fixed slice and inclusion**. It does not select a unique numerical map $D_7\to D_7$ or a unique phenomenal interpretation.

### The former splitting theorem {#теорема-расщепление}

The universal claim that $\operatorname{Map}(G,\Omega)$ canonically splits into physical and experiential predicates is **withdrawn [✗]**. Subobjects form a truth-valued structure, not automatically orthogonal vector subspaces. A projection $p:G\to B$ induces

$$
p^*:\operatorname{Map}(B,\Omega)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Map}(G,\Omega),
$$

with the opposite variance to the earlier alleged projection onto external predicates. It supplies no canonical retraction, product splitting or Serre fibration with an E-sector fibre.

A **conditional** replacement is valid: after specifying an observation map $o:X\to Y$ and a datum $y$, the homotopy fibre $X\times_Y\{y\}$ describes the compatible latent states **[T]**. Labelling this fibre experiential is additional data/interpretation; existence of a fibre does not prove a mental/physical decomposition.

### Different meanings of gap {#определение-зазора}

For declared same-variance functors $F_{\mathrm{ext}},F_{\mathrm{int}}:\mathcal C\to\mathcal S$, the natural-transformation space $\operatorname{Nat}(F_{\mathrm{ext}},F_{\mathrm{int}})$ is well typed. It is not a scalar phase gap, norm, or vector space by default. It has no canonical “zero” or numerical dimension. Its existence does not measure explanatory distance or establish whether one representation determines another.

Identifiability has a precise alternative: a quantity descends through an observation map iff it is constant on observation fibres. See [Gap identifiability](/docs/consciousness/hierarchy/gap-characterization#gap-инъекция). The pairwise phase statistic alone does not determine capability gates or all physical state features.

### No universal nontriviality theorem {#теорема-нетривиальность}

$P>2/7$ does not force positive E population or E coherence: a pure basis state outside E has $P=1$ and both zero. A literal E basis block has dimension one and cannot have mixed-state rank greater than one. A higher-dimensional experiential state requires a specified extension or conditioning construction.

Two complementary predicates do not imply that a natural-transformation space has positive dimension. Nor does Lawvere force nonzero self-model error for every state: $M=\mathrm{id}$ has zero error everywhere. The old universal gap theorem and its proposed proof are **withdrawn [✗]**.

### Two-aspectness is an interpretation {#теорема-двухаспектность}

A decomposition $G\simeq(G_{\mathrm{ext}},G_{\mathrm{int}},\phi)$ must specify a reconstruction equivalence and its category. One cannot define $\phi=\pi_{\mathrm{int}}\pi_{\mathrm{ext}}^{-1}$ unless the proposed inverse exists. Calling two declared descriptions aspects of one object is **[I]**; it does not follow from the invalid splitting argument.

### Consequence for the hard problem {#следствие-трудная-проблема}

The interpretive stance reformulates the question; it does not equate “why experience?” with “why does a subobject classifier exist?” as a theorem. T-214's former universal prohibition of an internal experiential bridge is **withdrawn [✗]**. Lawvere concerns a weakly point-surjective evaluator $A\to B^A$, whose presence forces fixed points for every endomorphism of $B$. An arbitrary internal map $W:X\to Y$ satisfies neither premise automatically; $W=\mathrm{id}_X$ is an elementary counterexample to the alleged universal prohibition.

The phenomenal meaning of an internal label remains an extra identification **[I/H]** because it has not been established by these mathematical constructions. This is not a theorem that a future formal or empirical bridge is impossible. See [corrected T-214](/docs/proofs/categorical/fundamental-closures#t-214).

## What can be unique {#структурная-необходимость}

### Conditional extraction and spectral theorem {#теорема-единственность-фв}

If an actual tensor factorisation $\mathcal H=\mathcal H_E\otimes\mathcal H_B$ is supplied, the reduced state is uniquely characterised by

$$
\operatorname{Tr}(A\rho_E)=\operatorname{Tr}[(A\otimes I_B)\rho]\quad\text{for every }A\in\operatorname{End}(\mathcal H_E).
$$

Nondegeneracy of the trace pairing proves uniqueness; the resulting partial trace is CPTP **[T]**. This is not a proof that a named axis of $\mathbb C^7$ is a tensor factor. Since seven is prime, a nontrivial factorisation with two dimensions greater than one does not exist at that size.

For a specified $\rho_E$, the spectral theorem uniquely determines its eigenvalues and orthogonal **spectral projectors**. Eigenvectors within a degenerate eigenspace are not individually unique. A selected projective quality model may use $d_{FS}([\psi],[\chi])=\arccos|\langle\psi,\chi\rangle|$; monotone metrics on mixed states form a family and do not force a unique experiential functor.

The former universal FV uniqueness theorem is **withdrawn [✗]**. Identity and replacement channels are both CPTP and metric-contracting, yet yield different state records. A spectral decomposition is determined after choosing a state; the choice of experiential lift, retained context/history, morphisms, metric and semantic meaning is not forced by those facts. A map on objects becomes a functor only after arrows and composition are supplied.

### Full-state information and content records {#fv-после-эрратума}

A trace-one Hermitian $7\times7$ matrix has 48 real affine parameters: six independent populations and 21 complex off-diagonal entries. A list of seven populations and 21 magnitudes has 27 independent real entries and is not the full state. Oriented triangle holonomies can retain some phase information; they and absolute pairwise phase gaps are different statistics. Their invariance depends on the declared gauge/frame.

T-213 does not prove that a channel output is a representable sheaf, nor that every state has a 138-bit description. Minimal Kraus number is the Choi rank of a specified linear channel **[T]**, not the algorithmic description length of its continuous coefficients. See [corrected T-213](/docs/proofs/categorical/fundamental-closures#t-213) and [reconstruction counterexamples](/docs/applied/research/reconstruction-identifiability#phase-counterexamples).

## Relational representation {#реляционная-идентичность}

### Yoneda in a declared category {#теорема-реляционная-определённость}

For a locally small ordinary category $\mathcal C$,

$$
\operatorname{Nat}(\operatorname{Hom}(-,x),\operatorname{Hom}(-,y))\cong\operatorname{Hom}(x,y).
$$

Hence isomorphic representables identify $x,y$ up to categorical isomorphism **[T]**. In an $\infty$-category use mapping spaces and equivalence. The category and its morphisms are part of the premise, not determined by the word “quality”.

In a metric space, the profile $z\mapsto d(z,x)$ identifies $x$: evaluate equal profiles at $z=x$. More generally $\sup_z|d(z,x)-d(z,y)|=d(x,y)$. If an $\varepsilon$-net has identical profiles for two points, triangle inequalities give $d(x,y)\le2\varepsilon$. These statements concern the selected metric representation. Interpreting all phenomenal character as relational, or excluding an inverted spectrum outside that representation, is **[I/H]**. Source and formal variants: [categorical formalism](/docs/proofs/categorical/categorical-formalism#enriched-yoneda).

## Self-reference and operational closure {#самореферентная-замкнутость}

### The corrected closure claim {#теорема-самореферентная-замкнутость}

A specified numerical self-map $M:D_7\to D_7$ has a well-defined iterate $M^n$ **[T]**. That typing alone proves neither that the system stores the output, nor that it predicts its own responses accurately, nor that an external observer is unnecessary. Canonical $R=1/(7P)$ measures proximity to $I/7$, not $\rho-M\rho$; a fixed point of arbitrary $M$ need not have $R=1$.

Use independent targets to test storage, first-model predictions and nonconstant metamodel predictions. The [canonical gate](/docs/consciousness/hierarchy/interiority-hierarchy) is $\mathsf{Cap}_2=(P>2/7)\land(R\ge1/3)\land(\Phi\ge1)\land(D_{\mathrm{diff}}\ge2)$ **[D]**, with separate higher-order certificates **[Pr]**. Passing these definitions does not prove the phenomenal interpretation.

## Axiomatic accounting {#минимальность-аксиомы}

### Comparing choices {#сравнение-аксиоматических-выборов}

Primitive count depends on how assumptions are encoded. One may bundle several independent assumptions into one sentence. Therefore “one primitive rather than two or three” does not establish mathematical or explanatory minimality. Compare explicit premises, deductions, observation bridges and predictive restrictions. UHM's experiential identification is one such premise/interpretation; its numerical model and dynamical choices are additional specified structure.

## Open explanatory work {#что-угм-не-объясняет}

The theory has not derived why a mathematical content record is felt, uniquely identified the experiential realisation, or validated a universal biological threshold. Calibration and independently tested structural predictions can address specified bridges. The [empirical programme](/docs/consciousness/empirical/overview) separates these tasks. Neither the withdrawn No-Zombie floor nor the withdrawn Lawvere no-go can close them.

### Frame and semantic calibration {#калибровка-как-g2-репер}

With state and semantic frame fixed, canonical $\Phi=(P-Q)/Q$ is independent of the site's topology $J$. Refining $J$ alone changes neither $P$ nor $Q$. A score over explicitly nested pair sets is nondecreasing because its added summands are nonnegative; it is a separately declared statistic, not a proof of phenomenal differentiation. See [T-210](/docs/proofs/categorical/fundamental-closures#t-210).

$P$ and canonical $R$ are invariant under unitary conjugation. Frame-indexed $\Phi$, E-coherence and phase arrays generally are not invariant under arbitrary $G_2$ transformations. A uniform real state $(1-t)I/7+tuu^\dagger$ and its rotation to $(1-t)I/7+te_1e_1^\dagger$ have equal purity but respectively $\Phi=6t^2$ and $\Phi=0$. Thus semantic calibration is not simply an undetectable $G_2$ frame choice.

<a id="faithful-g2-box"></a>
A proposed record is faithful/injective up to a group only if it separates the corresponding orbits. Observed marginals, eigenvalues, magnitudes or gaps usually have larger fibres; no finite-frame faithfulness theorem follows from uniqueness of the support reflector. The earlier claim that phenomenal equality reconstructs every physical state up to a finite frame group is withdrawn. See [corrected uniqueness scope](/docs/proofs/categorical/uniqueness-theorem).

### Physical versus effective coherences {#квантовая-природа-gamma}

Complex matrix entries can represent either physical quantum coherences or an effective information model. This choice requires a substrate/measurement bridge. If they are physical coherences, actual environmental decoherence must be included. If effective, their successful fit does not demonstrate physical quantum coherence or derive microscopic dynamics.

#### Scope of T-267 {#t-267}

A microscopic decoherence estimate for one physical substrate cannot be transferred to an unrelated effective matrix without identifying their variables **[C]**. Conversely, calling $\Gamma$ ontological does not prove universal immunity to thermal noise. Physical/effective identifications and noise rates must be tested for the chosen realisation. The impossible canonical limit $R\to0$ at fixed dimension seven cannot serve as a derivation of quantum mechanics.

### Identity through change {#идентичность-как-узор-оборота}

A process may preserve a selected invariant or operational identity while its microscopic contents change. State such an equivalence relation or conserved structure explicitly **[D]**. A fixed state, terminal object, stationary distribution and persistent cognitive agent are different notions; no general identity theorem follows by calling their common interpretation a “pattern”.

## Practical and interpretive boundary {#граница-карты-и-территории}

The proposal gives a mathematical vocabulary and a research programme, with exact identities at declared hypotheses. Clinical, taxonomic, meditative and moral assignments need independent empirical/ethical premises. A static scalar is not a diagnosis; iteration fidelity is not reflective depth; the phase profile is not a full state. The two-aspect identity remains **[I]**, and the empirical bridges are testable **[Pr/H]** where observations and independent targets are supplied.
