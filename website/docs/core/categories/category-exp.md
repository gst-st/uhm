# Experiential space: declared geometry and categories

A space of qualities and its interpretation must be supplied [D/I/H]. The symmetry group of a numerical state model does not uniquely determine a category of experience. The old universal T-42a uniqueness claim is withdrawn [✗]; [conditional RI rigidity](/docs/proofs/categorical/uniqueness-theorem#теорема-единственности) concerns comparison of specified complete encoders.

## Objects and spectral data

Let $Y$ be a specified space of experiential configurations. For a chosen register $\mathcal H_E$ one candidate quality space is $Q=\mathbb P(\mathcal H_E)$ with the Fubini–Study distance. Spectral values and projections of a state are well-defined, but a basis of each degenerate eigenspace is not. Lists of individual eigenrays require a gauge/quotient, and context variables require their own domain and topology.

In the native model an $E$ basis axis has dimension one, so $\mathbb P(\mathbb C E)$ is a point. A higher-dimensional quality register needs an explicitly declared extension/readout; a single axis is not a tensor subsystem for partial trace. The numerical existence of eigenvectors does not establish phenomenal qualities.

## Processes and histories

One may choose the fundamental groupoid $\Pi_1(Y)$ of paths modulo endpoint homotopy; all its arrows are invertible. Alternatively Moore paths with duration give strictly associative concatenation. Directed histories require a separately stated direction and allowable transitions. These categories are not automatically equivalent to continuous transformations or CPTP processes.

For an object readout $q:X_N\twoheadrightarrow Y$, a quantum channel descends to a process on $Y$ exactly when it preserves the fibers of $q$. On the collection of such channels the descended maps preserve identity and composition [T]. Without that condition, $F(\Lambda)$ is undefined, even if $q$ is Lipschitz. [Descent criterion and proof](/docs/proofs/categorical/categorical-formalism#4-функтор-f-на-морфизмах).

## Metrics and quotient constructions

The normalized Fubini–Study distance

$$
d_{FS}([\psi],[\phi])=\arccos|\langle\psi,\phi\rangle|
$$

is a metric on rays. Bures distance is a metric on states and is nonexpansive under CPTP channels. On full-rank states it gives the minimal normalized monotone Riemannian metric; **monotone quantum metrics are not unique**. Petz's classification gives a family parametrized by operator-monotone functions, unlike the classical Fisher uniqueness result. See [Petz–Sudár's primary formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0102132).

The formula $\inf\{d_B(\rho,\sigma):q(\rho)=y,q(\sigma)=z\}$ need not satisfy the triangle inequality for arbitrary fibers. For example, in four points of the line with fibers $\{0\}$, $\{1,2\}$ and $\{3\}$, adjacent fiber distances are one while the outer distance is three. Defining a genuine quotient/path metric requires extra structure and a separation proof. Bures contractivity does not supply those properties or a unique phenomenal metric. A product metric on spectrum, rays and context likewise needs specified factors, scales and handling of eigenvalue degeneracies.

For a chosen ray space the [enriched Yoneda theorem](/docs/proofs/categorical/categorical-formalism#enriched-yoneda) proves its relational identification and finite-probe error bounds. Its application to perceived dissimilarities requires experimental calibration; it does not itself identify experience with rays.

## Sheaf and homotopy constructions

For a supplied topological $Y$, its open-cover site constructs $\operatorname{Sh}_\infty(Y)$ [T], and $\operatorname{Sing}(Y)$ is a Kan complex [T]. This does not make a separately defined process category $\mathbf{Exp}$ a topos or its nerve a groupoid. An ordinary category has a Kan nerve iff it is a groupoid.

A continuous $q:X_N\to Y$ induces a geometric morphism of open-cover sheaf topoi via inverse images of opens [T]. Its physical and phenomenal interpretation is additional input. Identifying cognitive levels with homotopy truncations, or deriving physical time from invertible paths, remains unproved without explicit comparison data.

## Status

Ray geometry, sheaf construction and valid path categories are [T at supplied data]. The quality space, encoder, context and interpretation are [D/I/H]. Universal canonicity, a unique factor metric, automatic phenotype from $E$ occupancy, and unrestricted process functoriality are withdrawn [✗]. See [categorical formalism](/docs/proofs/categorical/categorical-formalism) and [measurement protocol](/docs/applied/research/measurement-protocol).
