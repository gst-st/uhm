## Before choosing a state space {#process}

Specify which preparations and devices can be built, their input and output ports, how they may be connected in sequence or side by side, and what record an experiment with all ports connected yields. A record can be an entire distribution of instrument histories. A single use of an unknown device occupies one hole in a circuit. Later devices may themselves implement adaptive policies if the laboratory supplies them. Extra copies of a preparation, joint effects and interventions with feedback each require an available device.

Two processes with the same ports are equivalent if replacing one by the other gives the same record in **every allowed completed circuit**. A state is the equivalence class of a preparation with no input port. This defines states before coordinates, topology, density matrices or a choice of dimension. Enlarging the laboratory can split a class.

## What is derived {#composition}

[T] Operational equivalence is preserved by serial and parallel wiring, on both sides. To see this, connect the proposed replacement to the rest of an experiment. The fixed neighbouring device together with that rest is already an allowed test of the replaced component. One can therefore compose the **classes** of processes exactly. Conversely, any rule identifying processes that permits those substitutions and preserves closed records can identify only pairs that pass every test. The resulting quotient is the coarsest such identification. Distinct classes are separated by the available tests in the sense that agreement on all of them implies equality. [Lean construction](https://github.com/gst-st/uhm/blob/main/website/research/operational-reconstruction/network/OperationalProcess.lean)

The premises supply the ports and the permitted wiring. Identities, associativity, interchange or causal independence in a physical realization must hold for its records. A law on representatives passes to the quotient; writing a pair of port labels does not establish the law. The quotient also retains joint preparations when the laboratory supplies them. Independent local preparations alone need not exhaust the joint state space.

For a joint preparation, the remote record is invariant under two local choices when both choices agree after discarding the local output and independent wiring obeys interchange. The proof allows correlations in the joint preparation and an arbitrary remaining test. Selecting the successful local outcomes changes the discard. A limit of physical networks requires its own control of signal propagation.

## A model that tests the strength of the premises {#alternative}

Give a classical cell an alphabet of $n$ symbols. Allow relations as devices, existential composition along wires, Cartesian product on parallel ports, and a possible/impossible detection record. The model satisfies associative serial composition and interchange. Every subset of symbols can be prepared and every equality filter can be measured. Two preparations are operationally equal exactly when they have the same support. Thus a cell has $2^n$ support states, for **every** finite alphabet size. Independent of this count, a single classical readout identifies each prepared symbol without error. In two cells, preparing equal or opposite fair bits gives identical single-port statistics; comparing the pair separates the preparations. [Model and proofs](https://github.com/gst-st/uhm/blob/main/website/research/operational-reconstruction/network/OperationalProcess.lean) · [Finite joint-readout controls](https://github.com/gst-st/uhm/blob/main/website/research/operational-reconstruction/network/ProcessControls.vr)

This countermodel bounds the conclusion from wiring and observable equivalence. The selected quantum realization in the numerical corpus takes complex density matrices and admissible channels as input (QG). To derive it one must identify further operational properties, verify them for the proposed primitive processes and construct a representation preserving composite experiments. A test for local tomography compares agreement on all product effects with agreement on joint effects. Real quantum theory gives a concrete competing composition law; in the two-rebit example a joint effect distinguishes preparations whose product statistics coincide. [UHM reconstruction and examples](https://github.com/gst-st/uhm/blob/main/website/research/operational-reconstruction/README.md) · [Hardy and Wootters](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.4870) · [Chiribella, D’Ariano and Perinotti](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6451)

Ordinary probabilities also admit a competitor. A finite tree of fair coin draws implements a stochastic device, with failed selections counted in the full probability. Sequential devices condition on the emitted symbol; parallel devices use independent draws. The complete closed record gives an exact rational probability. A cell can store and read each of any number of distinct classical symbols, and independent draws can be interchanged. Probability, closure under these compositions and exact records therefore still leave the choice of quantum state space open. The classical probabilities embed as diagonal preparations in a complex model of adequate dimension. An eight-symbol cell with exact decoding already exceeds the capacity of a complete seven-dimensional register under a common measurement. ## Why seven is a separate question {#address}

[T under specified operations] Perfect correction of one binary error with more than two codewords implies $N\ge7$. Length 15 satisfies the coding requirement too. On a separately specified signed interference circuit, visibility in both settings on every prescribed rectangle implies $k\le3$. If the same address register permits decoding all error positions and no error, then $N+1\le2^k$. The two bounds force $8\le N+1\le2^k\le8$. A concrete encoder and decoder attain it. Physical implementation of that interference test and sufficiency of that address are hypotheses. This selects the size of that operational address. Identifying the whole space of physical preparations with complex seven-dimensional quantum states is another step. ## Toward physical laws {#physics}

The network results start from specified local updates and compare signals after compositions and changes of scale. Locality on a discrete network can bound how soon a detector responds. Limits of different probes define a common nondegenerate distance under calibrated agreement, control of refinement errors, reciprocity and separation. A physical identification requires a network law that enforces these properties and an independent measurement. The limiting distances on the constructed rings and their products do not pick a Lorentzian spacetime, matter content or gravitational dynamics.
