# Consequences of the typed model

The consequences below follow from explicitly supplied mathematical objects. Ontological unity and the identification of matrix statistics with physical or phenomenal phenomena remain postulates, interpretations or hypotheses. The [kernel](/docs/reference/mathematical-kernel) and [premises](/docs/reference/premises) specify their interfaces.

## Topology, strata and cohomology

The density space $D_N$ is convex. The homotopy $(t,\rho)\mapsto(1-t)\rho+tI/N$ contracts it, so its ordinary constant-coefficient cohomology vanishes in positive degrees [T]. The nerve of the poset of all opens is also contractible because it has a terminal open; that nerve does not recover the homotopy type of an arbitrary underlying space. Sheaf shape and descent are different constructions.

At an interior point of $D_N$, the local pair is a real ball and its puncture in dimension $d=N^2-1$; its integral relative cohomology is $\mathbb Z$ in degree $d$, zero in other degrees [T]. For $N=7$ this is degree48, not degree7. Boundary/rank strata require their own local model; no universal $H^7_{\rm loc}=\mathbb Z$ follows from seven roles.

The rank-$k$ stratum has dimension $2Nk-k^2-1$ and support-Grassmannian homotopy type. Rank is a property of the state; a replacement toward a full-rank target can increase rank. No automatic progressive collapse of strata, arrow of time or spacetime dimension is derived.

The Bures open-cover site is genuine; a chosen metric tensor adds information beyond topology. Group actions, connections and curvature require their own fields/bundles. A phase Gap on one matrix is not automatically a holonomy or characteristic class.

## T-71: vacuum energy is an independent physical bridge

**[H]** A selected expression such as $\rho_{\rm vac}=c\kappa\omega_0(P(\rho_{\rm target})-1/7)$ is a model ansatz requiring units, coupling to gravity and empirical calibration. Even positivity of this expression needs $c,\kappa,\omega_0>0$ and a nonmixed target. The matrix $I/7$, a terminal topos object and predicate noninvariance do not supply those conditions. Nonzero self-model mismatch does not imply physical positive energy.

The former automatic cohomology/Lawvere derivation of vacuum positivity is removed. See [physical entropy and energy accounting](/docs/physics/cosmology-phys/origin) and [cosmological constant](/docs/physics/gravity/cosmological-constant).

## Octonions, Fano incidence and coding

Given a finite-dimensional real unital multiplicative normed algebra and nonassociativity, the octonionic classification supplies $\mathbb O$; its automorphism group is $G_2$. These algebraic inputs are not derived from autopoiesis or a subobject classifier. A chosen oriented multiplication and frame specify the seven-dimensional model.

The Fano plane has seven points and seven triples; every pair lies in one triple [T]. This selected incidence gives the Hamming(7,4) parity-check structure. Perfect binary single-error diagnosis with the stated nontriviality requirements yields the conditional dimension bound; it does not prove every functional system needs seven independent coordinates. Cayley–Dickson algebra identities concern the selected algebra, not an automatic cognitive-depth ceiling. See [minimality](/docs/proofs/minimality/theorem-minimality-7) and [octonionic assumptions](/docs/proofs/minimality/theorem-octonionic-derivation).

## Logic, dynamics and time are typed interfaces

The classifier $\Omega$ belongs to a specified sheaf topos. The seven projectors of a selected instrument belong to $M_7(\mathbb C)$. A GKSL generator belongs to the linear process model; a numerical self-model $M:D_7\to D_7$ can be nonlinear. The support reflector belongs to a slice $\mathcal E_{/G}$. These types cannot be substituted for one another.

Logical internality does not canonically choose Lindblad operators, regeneration, a Hamiltonian or a clock. The explicit Fano dissipator has its finite-frame symmetry; full $G_2$ covariance requires a separately specified generator. The former classifier-to-GKSL/time identification is withdrawn [✗].

Continuous-time dynamics exists even in finite dimension. A periodic clock records periodic readouts; it does not force the evolution parameter to be discrete or generate an irreversible arrow. A Page–Wootters constraint, a clock-system factorization and a preparation are additional data. Terminality of $1_{\mathcal E}$ does not imply relaxation toward $I/7$ or any numerical attractor.

## Composition, hierarchy and emergence

Two native registers have joint state in $D_{49}$, with marginals in $D_7$. Their joint state is not determined by its marginals. Product states, separable correlated mixtures and entangled states are distinct classes. Entanglement is not synonymous with coherence in a chosen frame.

For a specified tensor decomposition, von Neumann mutual information obeys $I(A:B)=S(\rho_A)+S(\rho_B)-S(\rho_{AB})\ge0$, with equality exactly on product states [T]. It measures correlations of those factors. Native $\Phi=(P-Q)/Q$, $Q=\sum_i\rho_{ii}^2$, is a different frame-dependent statistic. Assigning either one a phenomenal meaning requires a bridge.

Operational levels use independent task/readout certificates. A Postnikov tower has inverse limit with the relevant completeness conditions; neither its existence nor a chosen truncation forces a universal finite depth, L4 impossibility or an attainable phenomenal limit.

## T-54: a correctly typed fixed-predicate object

In the ordinary, 0-truncated part of a specified topos, choose $G$ and an endomorphism $m:G\to G$. Predicates on $G$ form the exponential $\Omega^G$, not $\Omega$. Precomposition is

$$
m^*:\Omega^G\to\Omega^G,\qquad p\mapsto p\circ m.
$$

Define $\mathrm{Th}_m:=\operatorname{Eq}(m^*,\mathrm{id}_{\Omega^G})$ [D]. The equalizer exists in the topos [T]; its generalized elements are precisely the predicates invariant under $m$. Precomposition preserves pointwise logical operations, so fixed predicates are closed under finite meets and joins (and the pointwise implication). This does not say every formula of a chosen formal theory is invariant: that is a separate condition on its axioms and interpretation. A numerical state map determines such a topos morphism only after a realization is specified.

## T-55: properness under predicate separation

**[T under a separating predicate family and nonidentity map].** In $\mathbf{Set}$, if $m:G\to G$ is not identity, choose $x$ with $m(x)\ne x$ and the characteristic predicate $p=\mathbf1_{\{x\}}$. Then $p(x)=1$ and $p(m(x))=0$, so $p$ is not fixed and $\mathrm{Th}_m\subsetneq\{0,1\}^G$. The same argument works for any declared predicate family separating the relevant pair. Conversely identity fixes every predicate.

This is a theorem about invariant predicates, not Gödel incompleteness or a universal Lawvere obstruction. A nonzero dissipator does not imply a separately supplied self-model is nonidentity. Nonidentity $m$ can have fixed states. Lawvere's fixed-point theorem needs its weak point-surjectivity hypothesis; Gödel's incompleteness theorems need a specified effective formal theory with sufficient arithmetic and consistency assumptions. Neither identifies truth values with energy or proves unavoidable physical evolution.

## T-56: theoretical self-application and ontology

**[H/I]** Reading $\mathrm{Th}_m$ as the world's internal theory is an interpretation. A finite list of model inputs does not prove finite axiomatizability of every invariant predicate, logical completeness, ontological exhaustiveness or preservation of dynamics under arbitrary theory extension. Replacing $m$ by identity makes every predicate fixed but need not preserve the earlier model constraints.

Monism, immanence, no outside and two-aspect identity remain philosophical theses [P/I]. A chosen mathematical realization can exhibit consistency of its specified constraints; it cannot certify every earlier interpretation or infer metaphysical completeness from autopoiesis. Incompleteness as a source of creativity is an interpretation unless a concrete update mechanism and performance criterion are supplied.

## Freedom and Hessian nullity

For a specified twice-differentiable potential on a $d$-dimensional configuration manifold, one may define $\mathrm{Freedom}=1+\dim\ker\operatorname{Hess}V$ at a stationary point [D]. Rank-nullity gives $1\le\mathrm{Freedom}\le d+1$ [T]. Under an invertible coordinate change at a stationary point the Hessian transforms by congruence, preserving nullity. The bound is49 on the full $D_7$ tangent space, or7 on a separately selected six-dimensional domain; these are different models.

Hessian zero modes can be lifted by higher-order terms. Nullity does not prove a manifold of distinct equilibria, free will, CPTP monotonicity or agency. Those claims require further hypotheses or interpretation. The earlier unconditional equivalence with the number of future trajectories is not established.

## Computational configurations and partial trace

Given a physical tensor factorization, define $\rho_{\rm comp}=\operatorname{Tr}_{\rm env}\rho_{\rm phys}$ [D]. Partial trace is CPTP and preserves trace and positivity, but can increase purity. For uniform $u\in\mathbb C^7$,

$$
\rho_{\rm phys}=|u\rangle\langle u|\otimes I_7/7,\qquad P(\rho_{\rm phys})=1/7,\qquad P(\rho_{\rm comp})=1,\qquad\Phi(\rho_{\rm comp})=6.
$$

The product has no correlations between its factors, yet the retained state has native-frame coherence. This disproves both purity monotonicity under discarding and “product implies zero native integration”.

For fixed $N$ and $\max_{i\ne j}|\rho_{ij}|\le\varepsilon$, the exact elementary bound is

$$
\Phi=\frac{\sum_{i\ne j}|\rho_{ij}|^2}{Q}\le N^2(N-1)\varepsilon^2,\qquad Q\ge1/N.
$$

Thus $\varepsilon<1/(N\sqrt{N-1})$ suffices to exclude the selected $\Phi\ge1$ gate. A bare asymptotic $\varepsilon\ll1/N$ does not do so uniformly in dimension: the PSD matrix with diagonal $1/N$ and equal offdiagonal $\varepsilon=N^{-5/4}$ has $\Phi=(N-1)/\sqrt N\to\infty$ while $N\varepsilon\to0$.

This bound concerns a supplied encoding and frame. It is not a theorem that all classical computers lack consciousness, intelligence or a capability level. Multiple realizability needs specified abstraction/realization maps, observation laws and resource budgets. A clock example of dimension $7\cdot6=42$ is distinct from a joint pair of native registers of dimension49.

## Spectral closure and energy

A chosen positive spectral functional can be evaluated on supplied operators. The eigenvalue scale, potential coefficients, observation map and physical energy units remain independent inputs. A fixed point of an algebraic self-model need not be a stationary state of the full dynamics or a minimum of a physical free energy. There is no universal spectral self-closure theorem from seven labels.

## 13. Conditional learning bounds {#границы-обучения}

T-109–T-112 apply to declared experiments, accumulators and admissible updates. T-113 [H] is a selected seven-role architecture hypothesis, not a prohibition on learning with $N<7$. See [learning bounds](/docs/applied/coherence-cybernetics/learning-bounds).

### 13.1 C24: forgetting under a bounded state speed {#forgetting-bound}

**[T under an explicit state-speed bound and POVM readout].** Let $\rho(t)$ be absolutely continuous, with $\|\dot\rho(t)\|_1\le v(t)$ almost everywhere, and let $p_c(t)=\mathrm{Tr}(E_c\rho(t))$ for a fixed POVM. Then

$$
\mathrm{TV}(p(t+h),p(t))\le\tfrac12\|\rho(t+h)-\rho(t)\|_1\le\tfrac12\int_t^{t+h}v(s)\,ds.
$$

The first bound is trace-norm contractivity of measurement; the second integrates state speed. If $v(s)\le2C\eta_0\|\sigma(s)\|_\infty$ is independently established, the selected stress-dependent estimate follows with an integral on the right. Under a constant upper bound it is $C\eta_0h\sup_s\|\sigma(s)\|_\infty$.

The former deduction from EWC and a Bures-adaptive step alone is withdrawn [✗]: channel contractivity compares two outputs, not an output with its unchanged input. The claimed upper bound of relative entropy by Bures distance is also false: distinct pure states can have infinite relative entropy. EWC alone supplies neither the required speed nor the readout law.

### 13.2 C25: identifiable reconstruction {#sigma-probe}

**[T under injective linear readout].** In a selected traceless Hermitian basis write $\rho=I/7+\sum_{a=1}^{48}x_aB_a$ and observe $y=Ax+b+e$, with $\operatorname{rank}A=48$. Then $\widehat x=A^+(y-b)$ and

$$
\|\widehat x-x\|_2\le\|e\|_2/\sigma_{\min}(A).
$$

For an orthonormal basis this is the state’s Hilbert–Schmidt error. Population scores are affine in $x$; clamping and the full stress panel are generally nonlinear. A full-dimensional local smooth chart needs at least48 output coordinates, but dimension alone guarantees neither injectivity, learnability nor $R^2>0.9$. Cholesky reconstruction is nonlinear and does not prove linear-probe accuracy. The former universal $O(D^2)$ sample guarantee is withdrawn [✗].

Check the observation law, smallest singular value and error on independent data; the [identifiability protocol](/docs/applied/research/reconstruction-identifiability) specifies that test.
