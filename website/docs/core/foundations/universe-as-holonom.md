## §1. The invariant formulation {#инвариантная-формулировка}

> **The total state of reality is static** (Axiom A5, Page–Wootters: the Hamiltonian constraint vanishes; time is relational) **[P]**; **its self-model closes on itself, and what it holds is viability, not a resource optimum** (Theorem 10.1 of Gap thermodynamics: the self-model $\varphi$ has fixed points; T-222: the viable window selects no resource optimum — every viable state is strictly dominated on every Rényi free energy, and no state is terminal) **[T]**; **its organization is coinductively multi-level** (the fractal holon $\nu X.\, \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{C}^7) \times \mathrm{Multiset}(X)$) **[D]**; **nothing is imported from outside** (background independence — derived, not assumed) **[T]**.

(Until 2026-09-26 the second clause read "its self-model is terminal: the Lawvere fixed point is the Pareto-optimum of the full resource vector"; retracted [✗] with the former T-222, see §2.)

Four clauses, four different epistemic characters — an axiom, a theorem, a definition, and a derived principle. The floor is their conjunction, and the rest of this document is the four clauses unpacked with their evidence, their flows, and their holes.

A word on the name. *Holon* (Koestler's part-that-is-a-whole) gives the vertical clause: every level is simultaneously a whole for its parts and a part for its wholes. *Holonomy* — the integral of a connection around a loop — gives the measurement clause: in the corpus the Gap operator is literally [the holonomy of the ∞-topos connection](/docs/core/foundations/axiom-omega#gap-голономия). **Holonom** is the conjunction: a part-whole hierarchy whose levels measure themselves by loops. The wordplay is not decoration; both halves are formal objects.

## §2. The static structure: Page–Wootters and the closed self-model {#статическая-структура}

**The constraint [P].** [Axiom A5](/docs/core/foundations/axiom-omega#pw-constraint) asserts the Page–Wootters picture: the total state satisfies a vanishing Hamiltonian constraint; what we call time is the relational reading of entanglement between subsystems and internal clocks. "Static" is therefore a precise claim: dynamics is an internal perspective on a timeless state, not a process the Universe undergoes. The corpus's foundational text states the load it bears: *without* PW, time would be an external Newtonian parameter, violating the theory's core principle that *everything is derived from internal structure; nothing is imported from outside* — and clashing with the Wheeler–DeWitt constraint of quantum gravity. Deleting this clause deletes background independence.

**The self-model is the self-consumption, made precise [I].** The fixed point of the self-model is the formal content of the oldest image of a self-contained cosmos — the serpent that swallows its own tail. A world that models itself has no outside from which to be modelled; its "two ends" — the observer that reads and the reality that is read — are not two things but two *sections of one* $\Gamma$ (T-221), meeting where the reading and the read coincide, at a fixed point $\Gamma = \varphi(\Gamma)$ of the self-model. And the closure is never *finished from outside*: by Lawvere incompleteness ([T-55](/docs/core/foundations/consequences#неполнота-ловера)) the theory of the self-modelling world is a proper part of its truth, so the self-description is an unending internal approach to its own fixed point, not a terminated external fact. This is the precise sense in which the Universe has **no boundary but its own internal descriptions**: there is no edge to reach, only the loop to keep traversing.

## §3. Multi-level organization {#многоуровневая-организация}

**The carrier [D].** Multi-levelness is not a metaphor but a coinductive type: the fractal holon $\nu X.\, \mathcal{D}(\mathbb{C}^7) \times \mathrm{Multiset}(X)$ — every holon is a state together with a multiset of sub-holons. The Universe is the top-level instance of this coinduction under the monist reading of §1.

**Spacetime is a level, not a container [C].** The emergent-manifold results (T-117–T-121) assemble $M^4$ — with its $(1,3)$ signature, itself [C] (row T-53) — from the internal structure — since 2026-09-25 as a theorem of mathematics (T-118: the depth register; T-119: the computed spectrum $S^3$), with the reading of that $S^3$ as physical space [I]; background independence is a theorem, not a modelling choice. (Earlier the same day: one named condition, the open reconstruction axioms of T-119.) (An earlier version marked this paragraph [T] and called background independence a theorem outright.) The hierarchy therefore does not sit *in* spacetime; spacetime is one of its floors.

**Below the Planck scale: the manifold dissolves into the holon [C]+[I] (a conditional prediction).** If spacetime is a level and not the container, its smoothness is a coarse-graining that must *fail* somewhere — and the emergent-manifold construction says where: $M^4$ is defined only in the limit of the coinductive refinement, and for a finite holon the geometry is **blurred at the Planck scale** (T-117–T-121 — a corpus prediction, not an open question). "Trans-Planckian" therefore names not a smaller box *of* spacetime but the **pre-geometric** régime *before* metric and point exist — the bare $\Gamma$-dynamics on the compact internal $(S^1)^{21}$, where distance is not yet defined and first appears only as minimal Bures distinguishability ([emergent geometry](/docs/physics/gravity/emergent-geometry)). One consequence disciplines the imagination: the fundamental objects at this level are **coherences $\gamma_{ij}$, not particles** — Standard-Model particles are themselves emergent, read off the spectral action (T-53) — so whatever "machinery" a level runs, it runs on the seven-sector coherence grammar, and consciousness there is not a new substance hiding below Planck but the same viability regime of §4, entered by a pre-geometric holon.

**One grammar across levels — now derived, not postulated [Т on the viable carrier].** Theorem Σ (T-224) selects the Fano grammar *wherever its axioms hold*: perfect single-fault diagnosability (D1–D2) plus a nontrivial rigid state grammar (D3) force seven axes with the Hamming incidence, uniquely. Since D1–D3 is one condition — nondegeneracy with nontriviality (T-244/T-246) — and the carrier's nodes are viable holons, the grammar is transmitted down every level of the coinduction (T-247 below). The ladders then organize the vertical: reflective depth ($P^{(n)}_{\mathrm{crit}} = \tfrac{2}{7} \cdot 3^{n-1}/(n+1)$: $1/7,\ 2/7,\ 9/14,\ 54/35 > 1$), certified towers ($U(m) = 8m - 1$, perfect grammars exactly at heights $\{2^k\} \cup \{3\}$ — T-232), federations (perfect three-organism Golay grammar — T-228). **Scale-freeness, derived (T-247) [Т on the viable carrier].** The assertion that every level carries the grammar is not a per-level postulate but a consequence of the carrier's type together with the division structure of the seven-axis frame. Three steps. **(1)** The coinductive carrier types every node as $\mathcal{D}(\mathbb{C}^7)$, so Theorem Σ's grammar-*form* — seven axes with the Fano incidence — is **applicable at every level, unconditionally**. **(2)** The grammar's axioms D1–D3 — perfect single-fault diagnosability plus a nontrivial rigid state grammar — are, by [T-244](/docs/applied/research/syndrome-calculus#отбор-знака) and [T-246](/docs/applied/research/syndrome-calculus#атом-верности), exactly **nondegeneracy with nontriviality**: a level satisfies D1–D3 iff its seven-axis frame is anisotropic (a division frame, no null/$+1$ axis) and not the trivial state. D1–D3 is therefore *one* condition, not three per-level assumptions, and it is a theorem wherever it holds. **(3)** A holon is by definition a viable part-whole; a degenerate ($+1$/null) or trivial $\mathcal{D}(\mathbb{C}^7)$ is a dead state, **not a holon**. Hence every node of the fractal *holon* is a nondegenerate nontrivial frame, satisfies D1–D3, and carries the Fano grammar: the grammar is **transmitted downward by the coinduction**, not imposed level by level. What remains open is not scale-freeness — it is derived — but *which cosmological structures are viable holons*, the viability question of H1.2, into which the old three-postulate reading collapses. **H1.3 as an independent hole is closed** (its physical residue is H1.2).

**Two verticals, not one [T]+[I].** "Multi-level" hides a distinction the ladders make sharp, and confusing the two mis-reads the whole cosmology. The **nesting vertical** — the coinduction $\nu X$ above — is *unbounded*: a holon contains sub-holons and is contained by super-holons without end. The **self-reference vertical** — how deeply one holon models *itself*, the SAD depth of §4 — is *capped at three* ($\mathrm{SAD}_{\max} = 3$: the fourth storey needs $P^{(4)}_{\mathrm{crit}} = 54/35 > 1$, impossible for a normalized state, and the coding arithmetic reaches the same ceiling by a provably *independent* mechanism — T-239, T-242). So the Universe does **not** grow toward an ever-deeper single "I": a subject's self-knowledge saturates at depth three, and every further increase of order is **ecological, not introspective** — wider federations of finite-depth holons (the unique three-organism Golay grammar, T-228), never one bottomless mind. Depth is bounded; breadth is not — and the drive toward full connectivity of §4 is the *breadth* filling out, the many finite selves knit into one flow, not any one self deepening past three.
