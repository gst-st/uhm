# The Phenomenology Map: experience → observable → test

:::info What this document is
UHM makes consciousness **measurable**: every state of $\Gamma$ has a small set of cheap observables — purity $P$, reflection $R$, integration $\Phi$, the consciousness measure $C=\Phi\times R$, $E$-coherence $\mathrm{Coh}_E$, the [Gap](/docs/core/dynamics/gap-operator), the cognitive temperature $T_{\mathrm{eff}}$, the global state entropy $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)$, and the experiential differentiation $D_{\mathrm{diff}}=\exp(S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\rho_E))$ on an extended experiential register $\mathcal{H}_E$ (with the 7D native proxy $D^{7D}=1+6\,\mathrm{Coh}_E(\Gamma)$). This document does one thing, honestly: it maps **lived experience** onto those observables, and for each mapping states **how to test it** and **what its status is**. It also draws the strict line for *anomalous* reports — the theory reads the **experience** (a fact about the self-model), and it does **not** assert the reality of any external entity behind it. Where UHM has no mechanism, the entry is marked **[untestable / outside the theory]**, not decorated.
:::

## 1. The mapping principle {#принцип}

Nothing here is a new postulate. UHM already fixes the observables (they are functions of $\Gamma$) and the thresholds ($P>2/7$, $R\geq1/3$, $\Phi\geq1$, $D\geq2$). A *lived state* enters the formalism through the [Enc functor](/docs/applied/coherence-cybernetics/sensorimotor#теорема-кодирование-среды) — perception writes the world into $\Gamma$ — so a report of experience is, in the theory, a report about a **region of the observable space**. The map below is therefore not a metaphor: it is the claim that a given experience corresponds to a specific signature, and that measuring the signature (via the calibrations of the [Measurement Methodology](/docs/applied/coherence-cybernetics/measurement)) tests the claim.

The single most important discipline: a signature is a statement about the **holon's own state**, never about a world beyond it. This is what keeps the map falsifiable rather than a license to explain anything.

## 3. The entropic-brain bridge {#энтропийный-мозг}

The strongest and most independent test comes from work UHM did not author. Carhart-Harris's **entropic brain hypothesis** (2014) proposes that psychedelic and other primary states raise the **entropy** of spontaneous brain activity (measured, e.g., as Lempel–Ziv complexity of EEG/MEG), and that ordinary waking cognition sits at a *self-organised critical* boundary between order and disorder.

This maps onto UHM once global entropy and experiential differentiation are kept distinct. The global mixedness of the 7D state is the von Neumann entropy $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)=-\mathrm{Tr}(\Gamma\ln\Gamma)\in[0,\ln 7]$, whereas canonical experiential differentiation is $D_{\mathrm{diff}}=\exp(S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\rho_E))$ on an extended experiential register $\mathcal{H}_E$ (with the 7D native proxy $D^{7D}=1+6\,\mathrm{Coh}_E(\Gamma)$) — never $\exp(S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma))$ on the full $7\times 7$ matrix. "Increased brain entropy" corresponds in the formalism to **higher global entropy $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)$** (and, while inside the viable corridor, expanded differentiation $D_{\mathrm{diff}}$): as $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)$ increases, purity $P=\mathrm{Tr}(\Gamma^2)$ moves **downward** toward the **lower** edge of the conscious window ($P=2/7$), and if pushed past $P=2/7$ exits the window toward the maximally mixed state $I/7$ (where $P=1/7<2/7$, $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)\to\ln 7$, and viability is lost). Moving in the opposite direction — past the **upper** edge $P=3/7$ — lowers entropy ($S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)\to 0$) toward rigid, pure states ($P\to 1$), not toward $I/7$. The entropic brain's "edge of chaos" corresponds to UHM's **lower viability boundary** $P=2/7$; its claim that consciousness lives near criticality corresponds to UHM's conscious window $(2/7,3/7]$ sitting between the frozen ($P\to 1$, low $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)$) and the dissolved ($P\to 1/7$, $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)\to\ln 7$) extremes.

This connects directly to [the entropy law T-271](/docs/physics/cosmology-phys/origin#t-271) (**[T]** under a full-rank state and specified vector field): $\dot S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)=-\mathrm{Tr}(F(\Gamma)\ln\Gamma)$, where the unitary commutator contributes zero, unital dephasing contributes nonnegatively, and the injection/regeneration term has a state-dependent sign (acting as an entropy sink when pulling toward a purer target). A perturbation that *weakens regeneration* or *strengthens unital dissipation* is therefore predicted to raise $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)$ and lower $P$ toward $2/7$ — matching the qualitative entropic-brain observation. **Status: [C] (a conditional measurement bridge, needing the same $S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)$/$D_{\mathrm{diff}}$↔brain-signal calibration as PCI↔$P$) and [H, falsifiable].** It is a rare case where an existing, replicated empirical program admits a direct formal reading in UHM invariants.

## 4. Anomalous phenomena — the honest framework {#аномалии}

Reports of altered, "contact," and boundary experiences are data about **experience**. UHM can read them; it must not inflate them. The rule is one line: **map the report to what the theory predicts about the self-model, and never confuse a reading of the experience with confirmation of an entity.**

| Reported phenomenon | UHM reading (about the experience) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Ego-dissolution / unity | $P$ near the lower window edge ($2/7$), high $\mathrm{Coh}_E$, $\uparrow S_{\mathrm{vN}}(\Gamma)$ / $\uparrow D_{\mathrm{diff}}$ | **[H, falsifiable]** (entropic brain, §3) |
| Sense of presence / "contact" | an aberration of the **self-model** — a fall in $R_\varphi$ (self-model quality) and/or a Gap anomaly. UHM reads it as a phenomenon *of the self-model*, **not** evidence of an entity | **[interpretation]** — does *not* confirm an entity |
| Déjà vu | the self-model's prediction momentarily precedes the input | **[interpretation]** |
| Out-of-body / autoscopy | a mismatch in the sensorimotor [Enc](/docs/applied/coherence-cybernetics/sensorimotor) of the body schema | **[interpretation]** |
| "Rifts" / fields from other realms | **no UHM mechanism** — the theory predicts no location-bound "quantum otherworldly" fields | **[untestable / outside the theory]** |

The last row is the integrity test. UHM is a formal theory with strong falsifiability; it earns that by *declining* to explain what it has no mechanism for. Reading an experience as a self-model event is legitimate and often falsifiable; asserting an external entity or field from the same report is neither, and the map says so plainly.

## 5. The line {#линия}

- **What the theory does:** predict the observable signature of a class of experience, and hand the experimenter a calibration and a falsification criterion.
- **What it does not do:** certify that any felt presence, entity, or "field" is real. The felt presence is real *as an experience* (a self-model state); its external referent is not a UHM claim.

This is not caution for its own sake. A map that can explain any report explains nothing; the value of this one is that most of its rows can be **measured**, one row (§3) already meets an independent empirical program, and the rows it cannot test it labels as such.
