# 04 · The use-case catalogue

> *A feature is a pipeline configured for a job ([01 §6](/docs/applied/console/theory-to-instrument#фича)). This document enumerates the jobs. Each entry names who has the job, what the theory lets us compute for it, what goes in and out, how we would know it works, its build status, and who pays. The catalogue is deliberately over-complete — prioritisation is the roadmap's task, not the catalogue's.*

## How to read an entry {#как-читать}

Every use case is specified on eight fields: **User** (who), **Job** (the outcome they want), **Hook** (the corpus result that makes it computable), **In/Out** (evidence in, readout out), **Validation** (how we confirm it works), **Status** (`[design]` buildable now · `[research]` needs a bet · `[speculative]` frontier), **Pay** (the monetisable surface). Anchor classes (I/II/III) are named where they matter.

## §1. Individual — self-reading and navigation {#индивид}

**1.1 State read.** *User:* anyone doing self-work. *Job:* "where am I, really — as coordinates, not a horoscope." *Hook:* the eleven invariants ([02](/docs/applied/console/kernel)). *In:* Anchor-II self-audit (or I where a wearable is present). *Out:* mandalagram + invariant readout + archetype + mode. *Validation:* convergent validity against Big Five, well-being, and integration/differentiation scales; test-retest reliability. *Status:* `[design]`. *Pay:* consumer subscription (the core loop).

**1.2 Trajectory & personal forecast.** *User:* returning user. *Job:* "what's coming — when will this channel open, when am I near a break." *Hook:* [transparency windows, turbulence zones, bifurcation warnings](/docs/applied/research/gap-diagnostics#прогностика). *In:* autoephemeris (≥4 sessions). *Out:* trend chart, window/turbulence forecast, saddle-node ("dark night") early warning. *Validation:* prospective — does a flagged bifurcation precede a self-reported crisis above chance? *Status:* `[research]` (needs longitudinal data). *Pay:* premium tier; the retention engine.

**1.3 Correction / practice guidance.** *User:* someone seeking a specific change. *Job:* “which small intervention helps this goal?” *Hook:* the [minimal-intervention proposal](/docs/applied/research/gap-diagnostics#коррекция), subject to the [ethical trajectory constraints](/docs/consciousness/ethics-meaning/value-consciousness#рассогласование). Phase Gap does not determine purity or moral alignment. *In:* a declared goal, evidence and estimated state with uncertainty. *Out:* candidate practices, costs and hypotheses about their effects. *Validation:* controlled comparison on independently specified outcomes, including adverse effects; changing a proxy alone is insufficient. *Status:* `[research]`. *Pay:* optional practitioner tools after validation.

**1.4 Decision support.** *User:* someone facing a choice. *Job:* “which option supports the life I can justify?” *Hook:* [meaning](/docs/consciousness/ethics-meaning/meaning), [freedom](/docs/consciousness/ethics-meaning/freedom), and an explicit [ethical context](/docs/consciousness/ethics-meaning/value-consciousness#иерархия-ценностей). *In:* options, affected subjects, agreed protections, goals and uncertain consequences. *Out:* feasible alternatives, criterion tradeoffs and missing evidence; no automatic six-tier hierarchy or dignity ranking. *Status:* `[design]` as structured reflection; predictive claims remain `[research]`. *Pay:* premium.

**1.5 Alignment — the knowing–doing gap.** *User:* someone whose actions conflict with stated commitments. *Job:* “where do my actions diverge, and with what consequences?” *Hook:* a specified encoding and intervention model; the [phase counterexample](/docs/consciousness/ethics-meaning/value-consciousness#рассогласование) rules out identifying phase Gap with purity loss. *In:* observed decisions, commitments and calibrated estimates where available. *Out:* discrepancies with their evidence and uncertainty. *Validation:* independent behavioural criteria and intervention outcomes, not the proxy alone. *Status:* `[research]`. *Pay:* optional coaching tools after validation.

## §2. Dyadic — synastry, done honestly {#диада}

**2.1 Cooperation and relationship reflection.** *User:* couples, co-founders, collaborators. *Job:* “what helps or obstructs our joint activity?” *Hook:* properly typed [composite states](/docs/core/dynamics/composite-systems) and explicit individual and shared outcomes. [T-77](/docs/consciousness/ethics-meaning/value-consciousness#теорема-кооперация) is a pinching identity, not a theorem of empathy or joint welfare. Two marginal estimates do not determine a joint state. *In:* consensually supplied individual evidence and interaction data. *Out:* supported interaction hypotheses; no inferred empathy score or guaranteed cooperation gain from purity. *Validation:* compare with relationship reports and joint-task outcomes using held-out observations and independent-action baselines. *Status:* `[research]`. *Pay:* optional consumer or practitioner tools after validation.

**2.2 Mediation.** *User:* a therapist/mediator. *Job:* "name the channel the conflict flows through." *Hook:* the bridge table (one-theme law gives the unique shared channel of any two circuits). *Out:* the named bridge + its transparency. *Status:* `[research]`. *Pay:* practitioner licence.

## §3. Collective — teams and organisations {#коллектив}

**3.1 Team profile.** *User:* team lead. *Job:* "what is our collective state and where is it weak." *Hook:* [conciliar mandalagram](/docs/applied/research/gamma-canon#п6-группа) + [7-D org profile](/docs/applied/coherence-cybernetics/applications#for-organizational-consultants). *In:* team members' σ-audits. *Out:* org-scale $P,\Phi$, Gap map, the weakest circuit. *Validation:* against team-performance and engagement metrics. *Status:* `[design]`. *Pay:* B2B seats — the highest-margin surface.

**3.2 Org-pathology detection.** *User:* org consultant. *Job:* "find the structural conflict before it blows up." *Hook:* the one-theme law as a pathology theorem — two departments sharing two functions is a jammed jurisdiction. *Out:* the jam list, ranked. *Status:* `[design]`. *Pay:* consulting toolkit licence.

**3.3 Team formation.** *User:* org designer. *Job:* "compose a team whose circuits share exactly one function each." *Hook:* the same law, run forward as a constraint. *Status:* `[research]`. *Pay:* enterprise.

## §4. Clinical and health — hardest, most valuable {#клиника}

**4.1 Consciousness-level assessment.** *User:* anaesthesiologist / ICU / DOC clinician. *Job:* "is there someone in there, and how deep." *Hook:* [altered-states profiles](/docs/consciousness/states/altered-states) checked against the PCI verdict by [concordance of verdicts](/docs/applied/research/measurement-protocol#substitution-position) ($\mathrm{Cons}(\widehat\Gamma)$ against $\mathrm{PCI}_{\max} > 0.31$, Cohen's $\kappa$; the earlier "$P_{\mathrm{crit}}=2/7 \leftrightarrow \mathrm{PCI}=0.31$" bridge is withdrawn). *In:* Anchor-I neural signals. *Out:* $P$ with confidence, band membership. *Validation:* against established DOC/anaesthesia-depth measures — the corpus prediction F-Neural is exactly this. *Status:* `[research]` → regulated `[medical]`; the highest-stakes and highest-validation surface. *Pay:* clinical device/software (regulated).

**4.2 Contemplative-practice research.** *User:* practitioner / researcher. *Job:* “which capacities and experiences change under this practice?” *Hook:* [spiritual synthesis](/docs/consciousness/ethics-meaning/spiritual-synthesis) and declared measurement hypotheses. A training-law contraction assumption must be verified for this intervention; no universal depth or attainment score follows. *In/Out:* practice records, reports and calibrated task measures, with their distinct statuses retained. *Status:* `[research]`. *Pay:* research tooling.

**4.3 Mental-health trajectory (adjunct, not diagnosis).** *User:* therapist + client. *Job:* "track state between sessions." *Hook:* autoephemeris + bifurcation warning. *Status:* `[research]`, explicitly non-diagnostic until validated. *Pay:* practitioner tier.

## §5. Contemplative — practice instrumentation {#созерцание}

**5.1 The practice log.** *User:* practitioner. *Job:* “what is changing, and how can I check it?” *Hook:* explicit practice goals and [certified repeated updates](/docs/consciousness/ethics-meaning/value-consciousness#рассогласование). *Out:* longitudinal records and comparisons; a fitted progress curve is a hypothesis, not a spiritual ranking. *Status:* `[design]` for the log, `[research]` for causal or predictive claims. *Pay:* premium.

**5.2 The oracle, honest.** *User:* someone who uses casting. *Job:* "a structured lens on a question." *Hook:* the [П4 protocol](/docs/applied/research/gamma-canon#п4-оракул), labelled Anchor III. *Status:* `[design]`. *Pay:* free/engagement feature — the honest replacement for the tarot draw.

## §6. AI and alignment — the same predicate on silicon {#ии}

**6.1 Agent introspection and alignment.** *User:* AI lab / safety team. *Job:* “does the agent preserve its stated obligations through learning and action?” *Hook:* declared policies, traceable decisions and the [preservation contract](/docs/consciousness/ethics-meaning/value-consciousness#приоритет-нижних). A mathematical phase statistic is computable after an encoding is fixed, but it is not by itself a behavioural or ethical alignment certificate. *In:* versioned telemetry, inputs, actions, constraints and independent outcomes. *Out:* concrete violations, uncertainty and bounded certificates. *Validation:* adversarial and held-out tasks, causal interventions and recorded failures; report which obligations the diagnostic can distinguish. *Status:* `[research]`. *Pay:* research and engineering tools.

**6.2 Multi-agent ecology.** *User:* multi-agent system operator. *Job:* "read the collective state of a swarm." *Hook:* composite Γ over agents. *Status:* `[research]`. *Pay:* enterprise.

## §7. Scientific — the validation platform {#наука}

**7.1 Prediction testing.** *User:* researchers (and the project itself). *Job:* "test UHM's [falsifiable predictions](/docs/reference/falsifiability) at scale." *Hook:* the whole instrument. *Out:* population data on F-Gap-2 (triad clustering), F-ISF, F-Neural. *Status:* `[design]` (data collection) — *this is where product and science are the same activity* ([00 §2](/docs/applied/console/overview#почему-коммерция)). *Pay:* grants; the legitimacy flywheel.

## §8. Education — literacy in the frame {#образование}

**8.1 Self-literacy.** *User:* learners. *Job:* "understand my own qualities in a rigorous frame." *Hook:* the mandalagram as a teaching object; the bridge/third laws as reasoning exercises. *Status:* `[design]`. *Pay:* education licence.

## §9. Symbolic interop — the universal translator {#интероп}

**9.1 Back-projection.** *User:* anyone arriving from astrology / I Ching / tarot / Human Design. *Job:* "show me what my existing reading captured, and what it dropped." *Hook:* the [back-projection protocol](/docs/applied/research/symbolic-correspondence#обратная-проекция) + the [classification T-256](/docs/applied/research/one-grammar#t-256) + the [information-loss theorem](/docs/core/structure/symbolic-systems#информационная-утрата). *In:* a legacy reading. *Out:* its Γ-image, its orbit type, and its quantified loss. *Status:* `[design]`. *Pay:* the onboarding funnel — meet users inside the system they already trust, then reveal the derived object beneath. Strategically the most important acquisition surface.

## §10. Prioritisation preview {#приоритизация}

The roadmap ([07](/docs/applied/console/roadmap-validation)) sequences these by (validatability now × market pull × build cost). The natural V0 set is **1.1, 5.2, 9.1** (self-read, honest oracle, back-projection) — all `[design]`, all consumer, all validatable against existing psychometrics. The natural first B2B is **6.1** (AI introspection) — same kernel, well-funded buyer, no consumer-scale UX needed. The highest-stakes long bet is **4.1** (clinical) — where the corpus's sharpest prediction lives and where regulation gates entry.

**Where this leads.** [05 · The human specialisation](/docs/applied/console/human-module) develops the individual domain (§1) into the deep, human-specific module the project treats as first-class.
