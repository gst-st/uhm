# Which physics follows from a network of processes?

An experiment begins with a preparation, an intervention and a recorded outcome. Two proposed mechanisms acquire different physical content when some specified instrument can distinguish them. The reconstructed core gives exact results about such comparisons on networks. The particle and gravitational models in this section introduce further assumptions. No derivation of the observed Standard Model, gravitational field equations or values of the fundamental constants from the common primitives is complete.

## The axiomatic core {#hierarchy}

The [operational state construction](../core/foundations/operational-processes) starts from permitted connections and records. The quotient by tests supports serial and parallel composition on arbitrary finite circuits. Which preparations exist and which dynamics the circuit implements are physical input. A classical family with arbitrary alphabet size satisfies these wiring requirements. Obtaining the complex density space and the admissible channels remains open. QG specifies that representation in the earlier numerical model. Perfect single-error diagnosis alone also permits a length-15 code. The [seven-address result](../core/foundations/operational-processes#address) needs a specified interference operation and a decoder sharing its address.

## How to compare a physical model with data {#cross-doc}

Specify how to prepare the source, what the detector records, which parameters are set by calibration, and which observation remains withheld. For a network experiment, one can calibrate clock units on reference paths, then compare first arrivals on different paths and probes at increasing scales. Record detection thresholds and misses. A probe crossing its predicted uncertainty margin is evidence against that shared response geometry under the stated conditions. Agreement with an input used to set an energy or a coupling supplies no independent prediction. [Network proof inventory](../reference/network-results) · [Physical premises](../reference/premises) · [Phenomenology ledger](../reference/frontier-ledger)

For example, the historical LEP estimate $N_\nu=2.9840\pm0.0082$ was inferred from resonance measurements using an invisible width and assumed couplings. Detector events, luminosity and efficiency enter that inference. Counting three harmonic pairs does not predict the measured cross-sections across the resonance. Those cross-sections with their calibration and uncertainties would provide a physical target for a new model. [LEP/SLC analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0509008)

## Next discriminating experiment {#open}

The first obligation before QG is to specify the allowed joint readouts of independently operated nodes. For two candidate preparations with equal local-product statistics, predict the records of one fixed joint operation, including unsuccessful attempts. Either the apparatus can access the distinction or both preparations remain indistinguishable in this laboratory. Recording this test and its alternatives before data collection would turn a choice of state space into a falsifiable physical claim. [Preparations and competing compositions](../core/foundations/operational-processes#alternative)

A concrete numerical target is available. For the two specified preparations of a pair of real two-level systems, all nine products of local basis probes agree. By linearity this covers every product of local real symmetric effects. The specified joint projector has acceptance probabilities **1 and 0**. This is an exact model calculation. A physical test would need independent calibration of both the preparation and the joint detector, an error model and held-out runs. Agreement would establish access to information beyond those product effects on that apparatus.
