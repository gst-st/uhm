# Confinement and the static potential

## A closed-loop measurement {#теорема-закон-площади}

Specify an $SU(3)$ gauge field, a representation for a pair of static sources, and a gauge ensemble. For a rectangular Wilson loop of spatial length $L$ and Euclidean time $T$, a transfer-matrix expansion with nonzero ground-state overlap gives $\langle W(L,T)\rangle\sim A(L)e^{-T V(L)}$ at large $T$, up to the chosen subtraction of source energies. Linear growth $V(L)=\sigma L+o(L)$ gives an area term in the logarithm. Proving linear growth, or measuring it over a controlled range, is a separate step. With dynamical fundamental quarks the string may break.

The stabilizer of a marked octonion unit supplies an abstract $SU(3)$. A spacetime connection, its action and the ensemble are additional inputs. In a supplied network with reversible transport, closed paths can already distinguish holonomies. They do not by themselves define a probability distribution over connections. [Cycles and observation](../../networks/cycle-observations).

## A scale for the tension {#диагностика-расхождения-σ}

In a trial model $\sqrt\sigma=C|\gamma|^2\mu_\mathrm{phys}$, the scale $\mu_\mathrm{phys}$ carries energy units. At fixed $C,\mu_\mathrm{phys}$, changing $|\gamma|$ from $0.047$ to $0.13$ multiplies the result by about $7.65$. Using a measured tension to choose either parameter makes it a calibration. A distinct test is to fix both parameters independently and predict a long-distance static potential.

## Chiral matter and strong CP {#тета-не-из-потенциала}

In a theory with massive quarks the invariant strong CP phase involves both the gauge $\theta$ term and the phase of the quark mass determinant. Real coefficients in a separate potential leave these data free. Gauge anomaly cancellation is another trace calculation and requires the chiral representation of all matter fields. These conditions cannot be decided from the octonion multiplication table.
