# Representations of the Standard Model gauge group

## Colour on one site {#теорема-фэ}

<a id="теорема-единственности-фэ"></a>
<a id="электрослабое-разграничение"></a>

The stabilizer of a chosen imaginary octonion unit $u$ is $SU(3)$. On a complex seven-dimensional site its action decomposes as $\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\bar{\mathbf3}$. These inequivalent irreducibles each occur once. By Schur's lemma every commuting complex linear operator acts by a scalar on each summand. The commutant therefore cannot act as a nontrivial independent weak $SU(2)$. Also $\mathrm{rank}(G_2)=2$, while the rank of the Standard Model group is four. [Octonion stabilizer](./g2-structure).

## A weak doublet on two sites {#кираль}

<a id="киральность-t327"></a>

Choose a common axis $u$, one colour triplet $W$ and a one-excitation sector. Its carrier is $\mathbb C^2\otimes(\mathbb C u\oplus W)$. An $SU(2)$ acting on the site multiplicity commutes with colour. An abelian generator acting by $q_s,q_t$ on the singlet and triplet is traceless when $q_s+3q_t=0$. With primitive integer charges $(-3,1)$, the lepton doublet transforms by $z^{-3}h$ and the quark doublet by $z(h\otimes g)$ for $g\in SU(3)$, $h\in SU(2)$ and $z\in U(1)$. Both actions are trivial exactly on the cyclic subgroup generated by $(e^{2\pi i/3}I_3,-I_2,e^{i\pi/3})$. The faithful image is thus

$$
(SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1))/\mathbb Z_6.
$$

The axis, triplet, weak action and sector were specified. This charge calculation supplies neither right-handed singlets nor a chiral field theory. On three sites the elementary exchanges on overlapping links fail to commute. A local gauge law requires compatible link and matter actions at every site. [Network tests](../../reference/network-results).

## The spinor construction {#sm-из-клиффорда}

<a id="поколение-t329"></a>
<a id="запрет-z-прайм"></a>

In a different construction one selects a complex structure on the real spinor of $\mathrm{Spin}(9)$. Its centralizer for the choice studied by Krasnov realizes the same global group. Identifying the spinor module with physical matter is an independent premise (Cl₀). Choosing a representation also leaves open the number of copies, additional singlets, the dynamics and additional interactions. Anomaly cancellation can be checked once the complete chiral matter representation has been supplied. [Krasnov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.11282) · [Bridge premises](../../reference/premises#t-350) · [Fermion families](../particle-physics/fermion-generations).
